Common questions

Do I own the website?

Yes. Once delivered, the website is yours. You own the code, the content, and the domain. If you ever want to move it to a different host, you can.

What do I need to provide?

Your business name, logo (if you have one), service descriptions, photos, and contact info. We send you a simple questionnaire after you order. If you do not have a logo or photos, we can work with what you have.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. The $497 is a one-time payment. You will need your own hosting and domain (typically $10-15/month through a provider like Netlify, Squarespace, or GoDaddy), but there is no ongoing fee to ScaleLocal.

Can I upgrade to a full plan later?

Absolutely. If you decide to upgrade to Foundation, Momentum, or Authority, we will migrate your website into the ScaleLocal ecosystem at no additional charge — and you will get the full dashboard, call tracking, reviews, and guarantee.

What if I need changes after delivery?

We include one round of revisions within 7 days of delivery. After that, text and image updates are $75 each. If you need ongoing updates, our monthly plans include unlimited content changes.
