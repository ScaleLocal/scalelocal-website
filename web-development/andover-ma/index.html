What technical quality looks like

The specific things that matter for an Andover business site:

  • Sub-two-second mobile load. Static-first delivery, optimized images, no plugin bloat.
  • Real schema markup on every page. LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ, BreadcrumbList — the structured data Google's AI Overview needs.
  • Semantic HTML. Real heading structure, real navigation, accessibility built in (WCAG-AA contrast, keyboard navigation, screen-reader compatibility).
  • Modern, secure hosting. SSL, backups, uptime monitoring — included, not extra.
  • Maintainable codebase. If you ever want to take the site elsewhere, you can.

Most Andover business sites built before 2024 miss two or three of those. The gap is where the more careful Andover buyer eliminates them in favor of cleaner-built competitors.

Two 2026 shifts that change the technical bar

Google's Core Web Vitals are now a direct ranking signal. A site loading in four seconds is genuinely de-ranked relative to one loading in one. The Andover buyer also notices the slow load even when they don't articulate it — they just close the tab.

AI search tools (Google's AI Overview, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) only quote sites with parseable structured data. A site without schema markup is invisible to those tools. Every site we develop in 2026 includes the LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ, and Breadcrumb schema baked in by default — not as a future "we'll add it later" item.

FAQ

Common questions

How do you know the site is actually fast and not just claimed-fast?

Core Web Vitals are measurable and we share the numbers. After launch we run regular checks — PageSpeed Insights, Lighthouse, real-user monitoring — and address regressions if anything slips. You can verify the site is fast at any time.

Will the site handle custom features or just basic content?

Most Andover service-business sites need surprisingly little custom development — the value is in clean fast structured content. When custom work is genuinely warranted (booking systems, payment flows, custom calculators), we scope it separately so you know what you're getting.

Is accessibility just a checkbox?

No. Real semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, screen-reader testing, WCAG-AA color contrast. The Andover buyer often includes people with accessibility needs and the site needs to actually work for them — not just claim to.

What happens to existing search authority during the rebuild?

We preserve it. 301 redirects from old URLs to new, careful migration of meta data, schema markup, and any existing structured content. Done correctly, rankings hold or improve through the transition.

How do you handle ongoing updates after launch?

Small content changes (new service page, updated phone number, swap a photo) are included in the monthly plan — usually turned around within a few days. Larger feature additions get scoped separately.
