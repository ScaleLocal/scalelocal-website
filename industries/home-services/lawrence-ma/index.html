The Lawrence home-services market is dense and distinct

Lawrence is one of the most diverse cities in Massachusetts with about 89,000 residents and a large Latino population. The housing stock is older — many three-deckers, Victorian-era multifamily buildings, and mid-century single-family homes across South Lawrence, North Lawrence, Tower Hill, the Plains, and the Arlington District. The result is constant home-services demand: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, contractors, masonry, drywall, painting, landscaping.

A meaningful share of the Lawrence customer base searches in Spanish or in a mix of English and Spanish.
