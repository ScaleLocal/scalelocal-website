## Before this sends

**What $997 includes, in writing.** The copy promises free deployment to a domain of their choosing and nothing recurring. That means they own or buy the domain, and the site goes on hosting that costs nothing to run. Decide before someone replies whether "deployment" includes pointing their existing DNS — because if the answer is yes, that's a support conversation you're doing for free, and if it's no, the email currently implies otherwise.

**The demo is `noindex,nofollow` on four layers.** That comes off as part of the cutover, or the new site launches invisible to Google.

**Physical address in the footer.** Commercial email needs a valid postal address and a working opt-out. You asked for nothing that traces back to you personally — the ScaleLocal DBA address satisfies that requirement while revealing nothing beyond what's already public in the Tewksbury filing. Company, not person. A registered agent or PO box works too. Worth confirming with your attorney during the v6 review that's already open; I'm not one.

**Deliverability.** Your standing rule is no links in cold email, with the asset delivered on reply. This puts the URL in every email because the link *is* the pitch. That's the right call for this product, but if `matt@scalelocal.net` carries your other sending reputation, warm a separate alias for link-bearing sends rather than mixing them.
