Returns prepared

Tax returns for individuals, trusts and entities

Three of the six services Dorfman & Dorfman, CPAs publishes are tax returns: “Tax Returns including individuals and trusts”, “Corporate Tax Returns”, and “Tax Returns for partnerships and limited liability companies”. They are described together on this page because one decision governs all three — what the entity is — and that decision then fixes the form, the due date and the order in which the returns have to be finished.

A limited liability company is the clearest example. A single-member company with no election reports on the owner’s Form 1040; a multi-member company with no election files Form 1065; a company that has elected corporate treatment files Form 1120 or, with a further election, Form 1120-S. The election, not the name, decides.

Where an entity issues Schedule K-1s, the entity return has to be finished before the owners’ personal returns can be. That sequence is the reason the entity deadline falls a month earlier than the personal one.

Extensions and lateness

Two rules that cost money when they are missed

The Massachusetts six-month extension is automatic only if at least 80% of the total tax is already paid by the original date. Below that threshold the extension is void and a late-filing penalty applies on top of what is owed.

Filing late is the expensive failure. The federal failure-to-file penalty runs at 5% of the unpaid tax per month, capped at 25%. Failure to pay runs at 0.5% per month, to the same cap. Filing late therefore costs ten times what paying late costs, which is why a return should be filed on time even when the money is not ready.

Partnership and S corporation returns are penalised differently and more sharply: the late penalty on Forms 1065 and 1120-S is charged per owner per month, for up to twelve months. A four-owner partnership that files five months late is charged twenty owner-months.

Assessment stays open three years normally, six years where a return omits gross income exceeding 25% of the gross income stated, and without limit where no return was filed at all.

Massachusetts

What the state return adds

Massachusetts taxes most income at a flat 5%, with short-term capital gains at a higher separate rate and a 4% surtax on income above a threshold a little over one million dollars, adjusted annually. There is no Massachusetts standard deduction, so a taxpayer who takes the federal standard deduction still itemises the state exemptions and deductions separately. Residents file Form 1; part-year residents and non-residents file Form 1-NR/PY.

Corporations in a loss year

A Massachusetts corporation owes a minimum corporate excise even in a year with no profit — $456 for the 2025 tax year. A dormant corporation that has stopped trading but has not been dissolved keeps owing it.

Depreciation does not match

Massachusetts disallows federal bonus depreciation and has deferred conformity to the increased federal section 179 limits for the 2025 and 2026 tax years. An equipment purchase written off quickly on the federal return will be recovered more slowly on the state return, and the two sets of basis records diverge from that point on.

Trusts and estates

Form 1041

Trusts are named in the firm’s own service line — “Tax Returns including individuals and trusts” — and a trust return is a different exercise from an individual one. Trusts and estates file Form 1041. Income is either taxed to the trust or carried out to the beneficiaries, and the trust instrument together with what was actually distributed decides which. The trustee, not the beneficiary, signs.

A first trust return usually needs the instrument itself, the date of death or funding, and a record of distributions during the year. Sending those with the first enquiry saves a round of questions.

Before transmission

Signature, copy, and where to go next

Under IRS e-file rules the taxpayer must sign the authorisation before an electronic return originator may transmit the return. A paid preparer must also sign it and give the taxpayer a complete copy. No return leaves this office on a verbal approval.
