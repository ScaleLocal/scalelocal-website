What We Do

Professional Pavement Solutions

From crack sealing to full-depth paving, we deliver lasting results with commercial-grade materials and 35+ years of hands-on expertise.

Sealcoating

Protect and extend the life of your asphalt with professional-grade coal tar and asphalt emulsion sealcoating applications.

Asphalt Paving

New construction and overlay paving for driveways, parking lots, and roadways. Precision grading and compaction for maximum durability.

Crack Sealing

Hot-pour rubberized crack sealant to prevent water infiltration, freeze-thaw damage, and further pavement deterioration.

Line Striping

Parking lot layout, traffic markings, ADA compliance striping, and custom stenciling with high-visibility traffic paint.

Micro Surfacing

Cost-effective surface treatment that restores ride quality, seals surface defects, and extends pavement life by 7-10 years.

Parking Lot Maintenance

Complete parking lot programs including patching, pothole repair, drainage solutions, and ongoing preventative maintenance plans.

About Superior Sealcoat

Built on Hard Work and a Handshake

Founded in 1990 by Robert Vita, Superior Sealcoat Inc. has grown from a small local operation to one of New England's most respected pavement maintenance companies. With over three decades of experience, we've completed thousands of projects ranging from residential driveways to major municipal contracts.

Based in Wilmington, MA, our team brings commercial-grade equipment, premium materials, and old-school craftsmanship to every job across Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Rhode Island, and Maine.
