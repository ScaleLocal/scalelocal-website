Our Services

Comprehensive solutions for asphalt paving, sealcoating, and parking lot maintenance serving Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Rhode Island, and Maine since 1990.

What We Offer

Superior Sealcoat provides a complete range of paving and maintenance services with over three decades of proven expertise.

01

Sealcoating

Protect your asphalt from the elements with our professional sealcoating services. We apply coal tar and asphalt emulsion formulations that create a durable protective barrier against UV rays, water infiltration, and oil degradation.

Sealcoating extends the life of your pavement by 5-7 years, restores the rich black appearance, and prevents costly repairs. Our experienced crews ensure even application and complete coverage on driveways, parking lots, and commercial properties throughout the region.

02

Asphalt Paving

From new construction to overlay projects, we deliver high-quality asphalt paving that stands up to New England weather. Whether you need a new driveway, parking lot expansion, or road construction, our team brings precision and durability to every project.

We handle site preparation, base layer installation, and final wearing course application using industry-leading equipment and materials. Our work meets or exceeds all local and state specifications for residential, commercial, and municipal applications.

03

Crack Sealing

Prevent small cracks from becoming expensive pothole repairs with our professional crack sealing service. We use hot-pour rubberized sealant that fills cracks completely and flexes with pavement movement, preventing water infiltration and further deterioration.

Early intervention through crack sealing is one of the most cost-effective ways to extend pavement life. Our technicians identify and seal all cracks before they expand, protecting your investment and reducing long-term maintenance costs.

04

Line Striping

Clear, professional parking lot striping creates safe, organized spaces and enhances property appearance. We provide parking lot layout design, ADA-compliant accessible parking spaces, traffic control markings, and custom stenciling for your unique branding needs.

Our precision striping equipment ensures straight lines, proper spacing, and long-lasting durability. We handle everything from initial design consultation through installation, ensuring your parking lot meets all regulatory requirements and maximizes usable space.

05

Micro Surfacing

Extend your pavement life cost-effectively with micro surfacing, an advanced surface treatment that renews tired asphalt without full replacement. This polymer-based system fills small voids, improves drainage, and adds years to your pavement's lifespan.

Micro surfacing is ideal for pavements showing early signs of wear but not yet requiring complete reconstruction. It typically extends pavement life by 7-10 years while providing improved friction, reduced noise, and a refreshed appearance at a fraction of new paving costs.

06

Parking Lot Maintenance

Comprehensive maintenance programs keep your parking lot in peak condition year-round. We provide pothole patching, surface repairs, drainage correction, and preventative treatments that address issues before they become major problems.

Our maintenance plans are customized to your property's needs and budget. Regular upkeep through patching, seal coating refreshers, and drainage maintenance significantly extends pavement life and reduces emergency repair costs while improving safety and appearance.

07

Bridge Repairs

Specialized structural repairs for bridge decks, approaches, and surfaces require expertise and precision. Superior Sealcoat handles comprehensive bridge rehabilitation including deck repair, waterproofing, joint sealing, and expansion joint maintenance for municipal and commercial structures.

Our bridge repair teams work with engineers and municipalities to ensure projects meet all structural and safety requirements. We use advanced techniques and materials to restore functionality, extend bridge life, and ensure public safety on critical infrastructure.

08

TechCrete Poly Patch

Our advanced polymer patching technology delivers superior pothole repairs that outlast traditional methods. TechCrete Poly Patch creates permanent, flexible repairs that resist shoving, cracking, and raveling even in harsh New England conditions and heavy traffic areas.

Unlike temporary asphalt patches that fail quickly, our polymer-based system bonds permanently to existing pavement and accommodates temperature fluctuations and traffic stress. One application provides long-lasting protection, improving safety and property appearance.
