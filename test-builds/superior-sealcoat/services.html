Crack Sealing

Cracks in asphalt allow water penetration that leads to base erosion, potholes, and pavement failure. Our hot-pour rubberized crack sealant creates a flexible, long-lasting bond.

Early crack sealing prevents water damage and extends pavement life significantly. We use professional equipment and high-quality materials to ensure proper penetration and adhesion for lasting protection.

Key Features

  • Hot-pour rubberized sealant resists cracking and separation
  • Prevents water penetration and base layer erosion
  • Stops small cracks before they become potholes
  • Flexible composition accommodates pavement movement
  • Extended pavement lifespan and reduced repair costs
  • Professional application with precise equipment
  • Cost-effective preventative maintenance solution
  • Works on driveways, parking lots, and roads

Problem Prevention

Water entering pavement cracks causes base erosion and accelerates deterioration. Sealing stops water at the source before expensive damage occurs.

Professional Process

Our specialized equipment cleans cracks and applies rubberized sealant at optimal temperature, ensuring complete penetration and adhesion for maximum protection.

Geotextile Fabrics

Geotextile reinforcement fabrics prevent reflective cracking in resurfaced pavement by absorbing stress and distributing loads. These engineered materials extend pavement life and reduce maintenance costs.

Professional installation of geotextile fabrics during overlays and paving projects creates a barrier that prevents cracks in old pavement from reflecting through new surfaces.

Key Features

  • Prevents reflective cracking in overlaid pavement
  • Absorbs and distributes stress loads
  • Reinforces compromised base layers
  • Extends overlay service life by 3-5 years
  • Cost-effective compared to full reconstruction
  • Professional installation with precision placement
  • Works with asphalt and concrete overlays
  • Improves drainage and base layer performance

Reflective Crack Prevention

Geotextile fabric creates a stress-absorbing layer between old and new pavement, preventing cracks from migrating upward and extending the life of overlay surfaces.

Professional Installation

Proper placement, tack coat application, and compaction ensure the geotextile fabric performs optimally to prevent cracking and extend pavement longevity.
