Design & Planning

Successful paving starts before the first shovel. We assess drainage, slope, and traffic patterns, then design systems that will perform for decades. For driveways and small projects, we recommend 2-3 inches of hot-mix asphalt over a compacted base. Commercial parking lots typically require 3-4 inches with proper base preparation and drainage systems.

Full-Depth Reclamation

When existing pavement is failing, complete removal and replacement is often the best solution. We remove the old asphalt and base material, test the subgrade, prepare a new compacted base, and apply new hot-mix asphalt. This approach is more economical than constant patching and provides a like-new surface with a 15+ year lifespan.

  • Extends pavement life by 3-5 years

The Problem with Cracks

New England's freeze-thaw cycles cause pavement to expand and contract. When water enters small cracks, it penetrates the base layers. Freezing temperatures expand that water, forcing the pavement upward and creating potholes. What started as a hairline crack becomes a significant repair expense. Crack sealing stops water at the source.

Our Process

We clean cracks with compressed air to remove debris, vegetation, and loose material. We route wider cracks to create a clean channel for the sealant. We then heat the rubberized sealant to 350+ degrees, creating a moldable material that fills the crack completely. The sealant cools, creating a flexible bond that moves with the pavement.

Bridge Deck Protection

Bridge decks face constant exposure to salt, freeze-thaw cycles, and traffic loads. Deteriorating concrete allows water infiltration that damages reinforcing steel and structural integrity. We apply waterproofing membranes that protect the concrete surface, extend structural life by decades, and prevent costly emergency repairs.

Municipal & Commercial

Reflective Cracking Prevention

When overlaying deteriorated pavement, cracks in the old layer eventually reflect through the new surface. Geotextile fabric acts as a stress-relief layer, allowing slight movement without transferring cracks upward. This simple solution can add 3-5 years to an overlay's life, making it one of the most cost-effective preventive measures available.

Installation Method

We apply a tack coat to the existing pavement, then lay the geotextile fabric across the surface. We apply a second tack coat, and then proceed with the new asphalt overlay. The fabric is completely sealed within the asphalt layers, creating a monolithic system that distributes stresses and prevents crack propagation.
