Our Story Since 1990

Three Decades of Family-Owned Excellence

From a small local operation to a regional asphalt specialist, Superior Sealcoat has grown by maintaining the same commitment to quality and integrity that Robert Vita instilled from day one.

Built on Integrity, Grown Through Trust

Superior Sealcoat began in 1990 when Robert Vita saw an opportunity to bring professional, quality asphalt services to the Merrimack Valley. Starting with a small crew and two trucks, he built the business on a simple principle: do the work right, be honest about pricing, and stand behind every job.

Today, under Robert's leadership, we've expanded our team to 5-8 OSHA-certified employees and serve residential and commercial clients across Massachusetts and the entire New England region. Yet the core mission hasn't changed. Every project—whether it's a residential driveway or a municipal bridge repair—receives the same attention, quality materials, and skilled craftsmanship.

The business has evolved to include specialized bridge technology work for public agencies, but we've never lost sight of what made us successful: being the reliable, honest contractor that homeowners and municipalities trust with their pavement infrastructure.

"Quality and integrity aren't shortcuts. They're the only way to build a business that lasts." — Robert Vita, President
