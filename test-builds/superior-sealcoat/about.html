About Superior Sealcoat

Over 35 years of excellence in asphalt maintenance and paving services across New England.

Who We Are

From Local Roots to Regional Leaders

Superior Sealcoat, Inc. was founded in 1990 by Robert Vita with a simple mission: deliver professional, reliable paving and sealcoating services to property owners and municipalities throughout New England. What started as a small operation in Wilmington, Massachusetts has grown into one of the region's most respected and trusted asphalt maintenance companies.

With decades of hands-on experience, we've built our reputation on quality workmanship, transparent pricing, and unwavering commitment to customer satisfaction. We don't just seal cracks — we protect assets and extend the lifespan of your pavement investment.

Today, Superior Sealcoat operates across Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Rhode Island, and Maine, serving residential property managers, commercial developers, and municipal agencies with commercial-grade equipment and specialized techniques you can count on.
