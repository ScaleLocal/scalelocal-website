Our Story Since 1990

Three Decades of Family-Owned Excellence

Founded by Robert Vita, Superior Sealcoat has been delivering exceptional asphalt paving and sealcoating services to Massachusetts and New England for over 30 years. We're committed to quality, integrity, and building lasting relationships with our clients.

Our Journey

Superior Sealcoat was founded in 1990 by Robert Vita, a visionary entrepreneur with a passion for quality asphalt work. What started as a small family operation has grown into one of New England's most trusted pavement maintenance companies, serving hundreds of residential, commercial, and municipal clients across Massachusetts.

"Excellence isn't just about the work we do—it's about the relationships we build and the trust our customers place in us every single day."

Throughout the 1990s and 2000s, we expanded our capabilities and service areas, investing in the latest equipment and training. Our crew members are OSHA-certified professionals dedicated to delivering superior results on every project, from residential driveways to major commercial parking lots.

In recent years, we've become leaders in bridge repair and advanced pavement technologies. Our commitment to innovation, safety, and customer satisfaction continues to drive us forward as we serve our communities with pride and professionalism.
