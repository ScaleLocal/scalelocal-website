Break-even volume and revenue

The number of units and the revenue at which contribution first covers fixed cost, together with the volume required to reach a stated profit.

Where contribution per unit is nil or negative, the fixed cost cannot be recovered at any volume, and either the selling price or the variable cost has to be revised first. Costs that are partly fixed and partly variable should be split between the two entries above.
