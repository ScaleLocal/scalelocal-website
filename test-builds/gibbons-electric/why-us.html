Three Generations, One Family

Founded in Medford in 1980. Tom Gibbons learned the trade from his father, runs the company today, and has kept it firmly family-owned through 45 years of code changes, technology shifts, and at least three "the trade is dying" predictions.

Mass Save Knob & Tube Sign-Off

Mass Save won't insulate a house with active knob-and-tube wiring — and your insurance company often won't renew the policy. We do the evaluation, the rewire (partial or whole-house), and the sign-off, all under one trip-charge.

Full-Service, Not a Specialist

Residential, commercial, industrial. Panel upgrades, EV chargers, mini-splits, generators, knob-and-tube rewires, computer networking, fire alarms, smoke detectors. If it's legal to wire in Massachusetts, we've wired one. Probably this month.
