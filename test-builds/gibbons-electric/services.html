What We Do

Our Electrical Services

Residential, commercial, and industrial electrical — from a single outlet to a 400-amp service upgrade or a full knob-and-tube rewire.

Six Most-Requested Services

The Work Medford Calls Us For

These are the projects that fill the calendar week in and week out. The full menu — smoke detectors, networking, fire and security, induction range hookups — runs to almost everything legal to wire.

Service & Panel Upgrades

Upgrades from 100 to 150, 200, or 400 amp service. We coordinate the disconnect with Eversource, pull the permit, install the new panel, and stay through inspection. Most upgrades are completed in a single day with power restored before we leave.

Request Free Estimate

Knob & Tube Rewire

Evaluation, partial removal, or full whole-home rewire of older Medford homes — many of which still have original 1900s wiring. We provide the Mass Save sign-off your utility and insurance company will ask for, and we'll work around your finished walls wherever possible.

Schedule Evaluation

EV Charger Installation

Level 2 (240V) home chargers for Tesla, Ford, Rivian, Hyundai, and every other major brand. We'll assess your existing panel capacity, run the dedicated circuit, install a hard-wired or NEMA outlet, and tell you honestly if you need a service upgrade first.

Get EV Estimate

Emergency Home Generators

Standby home generators sized to your house, your panel, and the loads that actually matter to you. Permitted, propane or natural-gas-connected, and tied into an automatic transfer switch so the lights come back on within seconds of a power outage.

Generator Quote

Commercial & Industrial

Retail buildouts, office wiring, industrial three-phase service, lighting retrofits, machinery hookups, and code-update work. We work directly with property managers, GCs, and business owners across greater Boston to keep projects on schedule.

Commercial Estimate

Whole-Home Rewires & Renovations

Adding rooms, finishing a basement, opening up a wall, or just modernizing an older home? We rough-in new circuits, run dedicated lines for high-load appliances, and trim out switches, outlets, and fixtures — with as little disruption to finished spaces as the job allows.
