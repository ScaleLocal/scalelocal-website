Our Story

It started in 1980, and we're still here.

The Gibbons family started this electrical company in Medford in 1980. Carter administration. New England in the middle of an energy crisis. And one truck, working out of the Medford garage where it all began.

Forty-five years later, three generations of Gibbons have run jobs in just about every corner of greater Boston north of the Charles. Single-family homes on Salem Street. Triple-deckers in Somerville. Industrial buildings in Malden. Tom Gibbons, principal owner, learned the trade from his father and has built the company into a full-service electrical contractor while keeping it firmly family-owned.

What hasn't changed: we still answer the phone ourselves, we still pull the permits, and we still treat every customer like they're going to call us again in five years. (Because they usually do.)

"Every job leaves my name on it. Whether it's a single switch or a 400-amp commercial service, the standard is the same."

— Tom Gibbons, Principal Owner

What We Stand For

Our Values

The three things we've built this company on for 45 years.

Honesty

We tell you what your system actually needs — not what runs up the biggest invoice. If a repair will do, we say so. If you don't need a full panel upgrade yet, we say so. No upsells, no scare tactics.

Code-Right Workmanship

Every job is done to MA Electrical Code. We pull the permits, we schedule the inspection, and the work passes the first time. That's how it should be. The electrical bones of your house should outlive both of us.

Long-Term Relationships

A lot of our work comes from referrals and people calling us back after 10, 20, 30 years. That doesn't happen by accident. It happens because we stand behind the work, answer the phone, and show up when something isn't right.
