What We Do

HVAC Services for Every Home

Furnace and boiler repair, central A/C, heat pumps, ductless mini-splits, and full system replacements — all from Andover's family-owned HVAC team.

Heating & Cooling, Done Right

Every System. Every Season.

From a no-heat call in January to a high-efficiency mini-split install in July, Gervais Heating & A/C handles the complete range of residential HVAC work across the Merrimack Valley.

Furnace & Boiler Repair

Fast diagnosis and repair when the heat goes out. We service all major brands — gas, oil, and high-efficiency furnaces, plus steam and hot-water boilers. Same-day calls get priority, and we'll tell you straight whether it makes sense to repair or replace.

Request Service

Central A/C Installation & Repair

From a refrigerant top-off to a full system replacement, we keep central A/C running cool all summer. Proper sizing, clean install, and an honest answer on whether your existing system has life left in it — no upsell pressure.

Get a Quote

Heat Pump Installation

Air-source heat pumps for whole-home heating and cooling in one efficient system. We walk you through Mass Save rebates and federal tax credits, size the system to your home, and install it right so you get the efficiency the spec sheet promises.

Heat Pump Consultation

Ductless Mini-Splits

The right solution for additions, finished attics, sunrooms, and homes without ductwork. Multi-zone systems give you room-by-room control with quiet, high-efficiency comfort. We size each zone, plan the line-set routing, and install clean.

Mini-Split Quote

Ductwork & Indoor Air Quality

New duct runs, modifications for additions, sealing leaky returns, and balancing rooms that never seem to heat or cool right. We also install whole-home air purifiers, humidifiers, and high-MERV filtration for healthier indoor air.

Ductwork Estimate

Heating & Cooling Tune-Ups

Annual maintenance is what keeps a system running safely and efficiently — and what catches small problems before they become no-heat calls in January. We offer fall heating and spring cooling tune-ups that pay for themselves in lower bills and longer system life.

How We Work

Our 4-Step Process

The same straightforward approach that's kept Andover homeowners coming back for over 25 years — no surprises, no pressure, no upsell.

1

Call & Schedule

You call (or use the request form). We confirm the issue, schedule the visit, and get a real person out — often the same day for no-heat or no-A/C calls.

2

Honest Diagnosis

We diagnose the issue, walk through what's actually wrong, and lay out the options. No vague "your whole system is bad" upsell — just the real picture.

3

Fix It Right

Repairs done with quality parts, installs done to manufacturer spec. We clean up after ourselves, walk you through what we did, and answer your questions.

4

Standing Behind It

Workmanship guarantee on every job, plus the manufacturer warranty on parts and equipment. If something isn't right, you call us and we make it right.
