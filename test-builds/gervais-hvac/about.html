Our Story

A family business, built one furnace at a time.

Gervais Heating & A/C started over 25 years ago with a simple approach: show up on time, diagnose the issue straight, and fix it right the first time. Two and a half decades later, that approach is still what defines us.

We're a family business serving Andover and the Merrimack Valley — not a national franchise, not a private-equity-backed roll-up, and not a phone tree that routes you to a call center in another state. When you call us, you're talking to the family that's going to be standing in your basement.

Over the years we've grown the team, expanded the truck fleet, and added new system specialties like ductless mini-splits and high-efficiency heat pumps. But the core hasn't changed: locally owned, honestly run, and accountable to our neighbors.

"We treat your home like it's our own. No upsells, no shortcuts — just the right fix at a fair price."

— The Gervais Family

Our Values

The three things we've built this business on for over 25 years.

Honesty First

We tell you what your system actually needs — not what makes the biggest invoice. If a repair will do the job, we say so. If a 20-year-old furnace is on borrowed time, we'll tell you that too.

Quality Workmanship

Our work is defined by precision, durability, and a commitment to doing the job right — so your system runs efficiently long after we leave. Installed to spec, every time.

Neighbor Relationships

Most of our work comes from referrals and repeat customers — that's not an accident. By showing up for our customers year after year, we build relationships that last for the life of the home.

Licensed & Insured

The Right Credentials, the Right Way

Everything we do is backed by proper licensing, insurance, and a workmanship guarantee on every job.

Fully Licensed & Insured

Massachusetts state HVAC and refrigeration licensed. Fully insured for your protection — liability and workers' compensation on every job. You'll never have to worry about who's responsible if something goes wrong.

Workmanship Guarantee

Every install carries our workmanship guarantee on top of the manufacturer warranty on the equipment. If something we did isn't right, you call us — we come back and make it right. That's the standard.
