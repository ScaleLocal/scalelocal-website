Meet Dr. Sarah Mitchell

Dr. Mitchell has been caring for families in the Merrimack Valley for over 15 years. After graduating from Tufts University School of Dental Medicine, she opened Elm Street Dental with one goal: create a practice where patients actually look forward to coming.

She is passionate about patient education and believes every person deserves to understand their treatment options — no pressure, no judgment, just honest guidance.
