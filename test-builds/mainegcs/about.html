About us

Built on relationships. Finished on promises.

Maine General Contractors — MGC to our clients — is a locally owned design-build firm out of Freeport, Maine.

Our mission

Change the status quo in construction.

Construction has a reputation problem: missed calls, moving budgets, vanishing crews. Our mission is to be the reason you stop believing that's normal — by fostering real relationships and delivering on our promises, from planning to punch list.

We build with precision, efficiency, and integrity across Maine. On time. On budget. Built to last. That's not a slogan — it's the standard every MGC project is measured against.
