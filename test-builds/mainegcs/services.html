Services

Everything it takes to build well.

One team for design, construction, and management — from a single-room refresh to a ground-up custom home.

Residential

Custom homes & new construction

From the first site walk to the day you get the keys, we build homes that reflect how you live — framed tight, finished clean, and detailed for Maine weather. House framing, window and door installation, siding in wood or vinyl, and every trade in between, managed under one roof.

Custom homes · New home construction · House framing · Siding · Window replacement · ADUs

Renovation

Kitchens, baths & whole-home remodels

The rooms you use most deserve the most craft. We remodel kitchens, bathrooms, basements, and whole floors with a design-build process that locks selections and budget up front — so the finished space matches the picture in your head, not just the one on the estimate.

Kitchen remodeling · Bathroom remodeling · Basement remodeling · Home remodeling · Demolition

Additions

Additions & extensions that belong

More bedrooms, a sunroom, an in-law suite, a second story — we design and build additions that expand your home's possibilities with style and precision. Structure, rooflines, and finishes are matched so carefully the addition feels like it was always part of the plan.

Home additions · Home extensions · Sunrooms · Decks & porches · Accessory dwelling units

Commercial

Commercial construction & fit-ups

Modern, functional commercial space delivered on a business timeline. Tenant fit-ups, renovations, and commercial builds handled with the same precision as our custom homes — because your opening day doesn't move.

Tenant fit-ups · Commercial renovation · Commercial investment properties

Management

Project management & design-build

Every project gets an experienced manager who owns the schedule, the budget, and the phone. We handle permits, trades, inspections, and the hundred small decisions in between — and we report to you in plain English, every week.

Design-build delivery · Project management · Scheduling & budgeting · Trade coordination
