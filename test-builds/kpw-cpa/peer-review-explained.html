What a peer review is, in plain English.

A CPA firm that audits does not get to be the only judge of its own work. Here is what that means, and what a rating of pass does and does not tell you.

Plain English

What a peer review is, and what a rating of pass means.

What is a peer review?

A CPA firm that performs audits, reviews or compilations does not get to be the only judge of its own work. Under the standards of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants, another CPA firm — independent of the first — examines how the work is controlled: how engagements are accepted, how staff are supervised, how files are reviewed before a report is signed. That examination is a peer review, and it ends in a written report.

How often does it happen?

System reviews under the AICPA Peer Review Program run on a three-year cycle. The report covers the year ended June 30, 2021. It is the report the firm publishes.

What do the ratings mean?

The report names them itself: “Firms can receive a rating of pass, pass with deficiency(ies), or fail.” Pass is the top of the three. It means the reviewer’s opinion is that the system of quality control was suitably designed and complied with, so as to provide reasonable assurance of performing and reporting in conformity with applicable professional standards in all material respects.

Who reviewed this firm?

Klesman & Company, P.C., Certified Public Accountants, of Palos Heights, Illinois. The report is addressed to the Members of Kolnicki, Peterson, Wirth, L.L.C. and to the Peer Review Committee of the Peer Review Alliance, and is dated February 28, 2022.

Why does the list of engagements selected matter?

Because a reviewer does not look at everything; a sample is selected, and what is in the sample tells you what kind of work was actually tested. Here the report states that the engagements selected included engagements performed under Government Auditing Standards, a compliance audit under the Single Audit Act, and an audit of an employee benefit plan. Those are three of the harder things a small firm can be asked to do.

Should this decide who audits us?

It should be one of the things you ask for, not the only one. A rating of pass is evidence about a system over a stated period. It is not a promise about your engagement, and it is not current information — this report was issued in 2022 about a year that ended in 2021. Ask any firm you are considering for its most recent report, read the period it covers, and read the rating.

Does a peer review cover tax returns?

No. Peer review is about the accounting and auditing practice. Tax preparation and planning sit outside it. That is worth knowing rather than assuming.
