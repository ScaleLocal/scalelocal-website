Membership

Two bodies, named by five of the six.

Five of the six credential lines on the firm’s site end the same way: member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants and of the Illinois CPA Society. That is the basis on which this site describes the practice as one of Certified Public Accountants.

Michael J. Kolnicki’s line names neither body. It names a Certified Financial Planner designation from 1999 and admission as an Enrolled Agent in 2017. Those are different credentials, and they are not treated here as if they were the same.

Ask a partner what the work actually involves.

Five of the six people here are Certified Public Accountants, and the one you speak to is the one who does the work. Say what you have; the answer will be a scope, not a brochure.
