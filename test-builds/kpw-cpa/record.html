A note on arithmetic

Why there is no number of years anywhere on this site.

The firm was started in 1974. That is a fact with a date on it, and it will still be true in ten years.

A count of years is not that. It is a fact with an expiry date, and on a website nobody rewrites it goes quietly wrong. The firm’s current site carries a line about experience that its own founding year contradicts — which is exactly how these things fail.

So this site gives dates and lets you do the subtraction. Every year on this page is set in tabular figures for the same reason a ledger is: so the columns line up and a number can be read against its neighbor.

Where it comes from

Sources.

Founding year, tenures, degree years, certificate years and prior firms are taken from the firm’s own About page. The 2021 and 2022 entries are taken from the peer review report the firm publishes, which is reproduced in full on this site.

  • The firm’s own statement: “Kolnicki, Peterson & Wirth, LLC is a Downers Grove based firm that was started in 1974.”
  • Each partner’s own education, membership and experience lines.
  • The peer review report of Klesman & Company, P.C., dated February 28, 2022.
