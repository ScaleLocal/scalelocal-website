Questions

Nine that come up first.

Answered from what the firm publishes. Where the honest answer is that the firm publishes nothing, that is the answer given.

Which do I need — an audit, a review, or a compilation?

Whoever asked for the statements decides. A bank, a bonding company, a grantor or a regulator will normally name the level of service in writing. Bring that letter to the first conversation; it saves a great deal of guessing and it is the single biggest driver of what the work costs. The firm provides all three.

What does the peer review rating actually cover?

The firm’s accounting and auditing practice, for the year ended June 30, 2021. The report is dated February 28, 2022, was issued by Klesman & Company, P.C., and gives a rating of pass. It says nothing about later periods and nothing about tax work.

Do you audit governmental bodies and non-profit organizations?

Two partners, Kenneth W. Peterson and Michael J. Eckel, state audit services to governmental and non-profit organizations as their competence. The peer review sample supports it: the engagements selected included work performed under Government Auditing Standards and a compliance audit under the Single Audit Act.

Do you audit employee benefit plans?

The peer review report states that the engagements selected for review included an audit of an employee benefit plan. That is the firm’s own published evidence of the work, and it is a more specific statement than most firms can make.

Is everyone at the firm a CPA?

No, and the firm does not claim it. Five of the six are Certified Public Accountants, each with an Illinois CPA Certificate dated between 1971 and 1984. Michael J. Kolnicki is a Certified Financial Planner from 1999 and an Enrolled Agent from 2017; the firm gives him no other title and neither does this site.

How long has the firm been going?

It was started in 1974. This site gives the year rather than a count of years, because a count goes stale the moment it is written and nobody ever goes back to update it. The chronology page lists every other date the firm publishes.

Can I work with one particular person?

The firm publishes an individual mailbox for each of the six people, and each person’s page here lists the competences that person states. If the work is clearly one person’s, say so when you call.

Can I send documents or settle a bill through this website?

No. The firm publishes no facility for either, so this site does not pretend otherwise. Contact is by telephone, by email to one of the published mailboxes, or in person at 1400 Opus Place. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm; the office is closed Saturday and Sunday.

What should I bring to a first conversation?

Whatever prompted it. The letter from the lender or the grantor, the last set of statements, the last return filed, and a rough idea of the deadline you are working to. A scope can be described from those four things.
