The report

Klesman & Company, P.C., February 28, 2022.

Reproduced from the report the firm publishes. The three quoted passages are the scope paragraph, the note on engagements selected, and the opinion.

Klesman & Company, P.C.

Certified Public Accountants · Palos Heights, Illinois
Letter dated February 28, 2022
To the Members of Kolnicki, Peterson, Wirth, L.L.C. and the Peer Review Committee of the Peer Review Alliance

“We have reviewed the system of quality control for the accounting and auditing practice of Kolnicki, Peterson, Wirth, L.L.C. (the firm) in effect for the year ended June 30, 2021. Our peer review was conducted in accordance with the Standards for Performing and Reporting on Peer Reviews established by the Peer Review Board of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (Standards).”

“Engagements selected for review included engagements performed under Government Auditing Standards, a compliance audit under the Single Audit Act, and an audit of an employee benefit plan.”

“In our opinion, the system of quality control for the accounting and auditing practice of Kolnicki, Peterson, Wirth, L.L.C. in effect for the year ended June 30, 2021, has been suitably designed and complied with to provide the firm with reasonable assurance of performing and reporting in conformity with applicable professional standards in all material respects. Firms can receive a rating of pass, pass with deficiency(ies), or fail. Kolnicki, Peterson, Wirth, L.L.C. has received a peer review rating of pass.”

Rating — Pass · Period reviewed — year ended June 30, 2021

Why this is here as text

The firm publishes this report on its own site as a photograph of a sheet of paper. A photograph cannot be searched, cannot be quoted, cannot be read aloud by a screen reader and cannot be found by anyone looking for a reviewed firm. The words above are the same words, set as text.

Being precise about it

What this report says, and what it does not.

The value of a document like this is exactly proportional to how carefully it is described.

It says that for the year ended June 30, 2021 the firm’s system of quality control over its accounting and auditing practice was suitably designed and complied with, and that the rating is pass. That is the highest of the three ratings the report itself names.

It does not say anything about any period after June 30, 2021. It is not a statement about the firm today, and nothing on this page should be read as one. If the position since matters to you — and for an audit engagement it should — ask the firm directly, and ask for the report itself rather than a summary of it.

It also says nothing about tax work. Peer review covers the accounting and auditing practice: audits, reviews and compilations. A tax return is not within its scope.
