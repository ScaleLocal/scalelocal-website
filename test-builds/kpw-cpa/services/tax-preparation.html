Tax preparation.

Planning through the year and forecasts of year-end liabilities, rather than a few meetings when it comes time to file.

The firm’s words

Year-round, not once a year.

Quoted from the firm’s own tax preparation page.

“Maximizing your after-tax earnings is your major objective, and you get year-round planning assistance from our tax services — not just a few meetings when it comes time to file your returns… You will receive forecasts of year-end tax liabilities so that you will have adequate time to minimize their impact through reductions and/or deferments.”

Why the timing is the point

A forecast is only useful while you can still act.

The distinction the firm draws is between planning and filing, and it puts the weight on the first.

Almost everything that changes a tax outcome has to happen before the year closes. Once the books are shut, a preparer is reporting decisions, not shaping them. That is why the sentence above talks about forecasts of year-end liabilities rather than about filing.

The firm names tax planning as a competence of four of its partners, and names income tax return preparation — individual and business — among its stated strengths. Estate planning is named too, by the firm generally and by Richard D. Wirth in particular.
