Accounting services.

Three levels of financial statement service — audit, review, and compilation — and a peer-reviewed system of quality control behind them.

The firm’s words

Three levels of financial statement service.

Quoted from the firm’s own accounting services page.

“Depending upon the extent of your needs, Kolnicki, Peterson, Wirth LLC provide three levels of financial statement services — audit, review, and compilation.”

That sentence is the whole offer, and it is worth reading slowly. The three are not grades of care. They are three different sets of procedures producing three different reports, and the one you need is decided by whoever is going to read the statements.

The audit approach

Nine things the firm states its audit includes.

Set out in the firm’s own order and its own wording.

  1. Reviewing and evaluating your accounting system
  2. Reviewing and evaluating current procedures and controls
  3. Help with controlling inventory
  4. Testing compliance with manufacturers’ selling agreements and management contracts
  5. Confirmation with outside parties
  6. Physical inspection and observation
  7. Tracing transactions to supporting documents
  8. Reviewing and evaluating cash management procedures
  9. Reviewing and evaluating compensation policies

And then a letter

“Our auditing team also searches for potential problems, such as waste and nonproductive assets, that affect profitability. We meet with you personally to discuss our findings, and then provide you with a written report in the form of a management letter.”

The management letter is the part clients tend to remember. It is where the observations that are not required to be in the opinion end up.

Quality control

Reviewed, and rated pass.

The one part of a CPA firm’s work that is independently examined is this one.

The accounting and auditing practice described on this page is the part of the firm that a peer review examines. Klesman & Company, P.C. reviewed it for the year ended June 30, 2021 and issued a report on February 28, 2022 rating it pass. The engagements selected for that review included work performed under Government Auditing Standards, a compliance audit under the Single Audit Act, and an audit of an employee benefit plan.

The report is reproduced in full on this site, as text.
