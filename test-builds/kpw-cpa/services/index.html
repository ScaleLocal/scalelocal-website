Language

Audit, review, and compilation.

The firm’s own words are “three levels of financial statement services — audit, review, and compilation.” This site uses those three words and does not reach for a broader one. They are not interchangeable, they cost very different amounts, and the difference between them is the single most useful thing to establish before anyone quotes for work.

  • An audit is the highest level: procedures performed to support an opinion on the statements.
  • A review is a narrower set of procedures, and the report that results says considerably less than an audit report does.
  • A compilation presents information supplied by management in the form of financial statements, without either.

Which one you need is usually decided by whoever asked for the statements — a lender, a bonding company, a grantor, a regulator. Ask them which one, and bring the answer.
