The differentiator

If a letter has arrived, start here.

The firm lists federal and state representation among its services. This page sets out what that means, who is permitted to do it, and exactly what the firm publishes about its own two people.

“Federal and State Representation” sits on this firm’s capability list, and “IRS & state representation” is a heading on its income tax page. For a two-person office that is a more substantial offer than it looks, and it is worth being precise about why.

Who the IRS lets represent you

The IRS grants unlimited representation rights before it to three groups: attorneys, certified public accountants, and enrolled agents. Everyone else is either limited or not permitted to represent you at all. That is the rule, not the firm’s claim about itself.

What the firm publishes about its own people

Tom Craig’s bio records that he “became an Enrolled Agent, was licensed to sell securities and insurance products and became licensed as a Certified Public Accountant.” Two of the three groups above, in one person, in a practice of two.

Joe Brine’s bio says one thing about credentials, and this site says no more than it does:

He also continued his education by pursuing Enrolled Agent status (Licensed by the Internal Revenue Service).The firm’s own sentence, quoted in full. It says pursuing; it does not say completed, so neither does this page.

What to do when the envelope arrives

Most notices are machine-generated, a good number of them are wrong, and almost all of them carry a date that governs everything that happens next.

  1. Read the response date before you read anything else. It is usually 30 days, sometimes 90, and it is not negotiable after it passes.
  2. Keep the envelope with the letter. The notice number and the tax year printed on it decide what kind of matter this is.
  3. Do not pay it because paying is easier. A notice is a proposal, not a finding, and agreeing to one you do not owe is expensive to unwind.
  4. Call before the date, not after it. Almost every option available to you is wider while the clock is still running.

Massachusetts too

A Massachusetts Department of Revenue notice runs on its own timetable and is not answered by anything you send the IRS. The firm’s list says federal and state, and that distinction is the reason the word “and” is in it.

The honest limits

This page is about a service the firm advertises. It is not a promise about an outcome.

Representation means someone stands between you and the agency: they receive the correspondence, they respond to it, and they attend on your behalf. It does not mean a liability disappears, and nobody who tells you otherwise before reading your file is worth listening to.

What it does buy is that the response is written by someone who has read the notice properly, and that the deadline is met.
