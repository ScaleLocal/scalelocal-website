Four steps to intentional success.

That is the firm’s own framing, and it is worth taking literally. The practice is organized as four services rather than one undifferentiated “accounting” offer, and they are numbered because they stack.

“Any 1 step gets you on your way. More than 1 helps you arrive!” — the firm’s words, on its own site.

What the practice actually offers

No expansion, no rewording. This is the capability list as the firm publishes it, with one typo corrected.

  • Tax Planning and Preparation

That list is the firm’s own, printed on its site in those words, and it ends “…and much more.”

Worth stating plainly

The firm advertises compilation of financial statements and nothing beyond it. In its own words, a compilation is “based on client information provided and used primarily for internal use only, as no assurance is expressed.” If a lender has asked you for something more than that, the office will tell you so rather than take the engagement.

If a letter has arrived, this is the part that matters

Two of the credentials the IRS recognizes for unlimited practice rights — Certified Public Accountant and Enrolled Agent — are held by one person in this two-person office. “Federal and State Representation” is on the firm’s own service list, and “IRS & state representation” is a heading on its income tax page.
