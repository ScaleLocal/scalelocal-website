Income Tax Services.

Step one, and the one most people arrive for. Planning and preparation on one side, representation on the other.

Tax planning & return preparation

In the firm’s own words: “Our state-of-the art software accurately prepares returns for individuals, corporations, partnerships, LLCs/LLPs, trusts, and estates. We advise on divorce and support issues and the tax effects of buying/selling a home or business.”

Planning is the half that has to happen before the year closes. A return records decisions that were already made; the useful conversation is the one that happens while they can still be made differently.

Estate and gift

Estate and gift planning is named on the firm’s income tax page and again under Financial Services, where comprehensive estate planning, gift planning, family wealth preservation and trust administration sit together.

Who it is forThe federal return that normally applies
An individual or a coupleForm 1040, with the Massachusetts return alongside it
A corporationForm 1120, or Form 1120-S where an S election is in place
A partnership, LLC or LLPForm 1065, with a Schedule K-1 to each partner or member
A trust or an estateForm 1041

Which return an entity files depends on how it is organized and on elections already made. Entity selection is itself on the firm’s accounting services list.

IRS & state representation

That heading is the firm’s, and “Federal and State Representation” appears on its capability list. If a notice has arrived from the IRS or from the Massachusetts Department of Revenue, that is the service you are looking for.

Two things to bring

The notice itself, front and back, including the response date printed on it. And the return the notice is about, if you have it. Most of what happens next is decided by that date.
