Financial Services.

Step two. The decisions that are not annual, reviewed on a schedule instead of once.

The firm groups this work around financial and retirement decisions with periodic reviews — the point being that a plan set once and never looked at again is a plan that has quietly stopped matching the facts.

What is on the list

  • Financial and retirement decisions, with periodic reviews
  • Comprehensive estate planning and financial analysis
  • Gift planning
  • Family wealth preservation
  • Trust administration
  • Pension and profit sharing plans
  • College funding strategies

College funding, specifically

The firm names 529 plans, FAFSA assistance, Coverdell education savings accounts and qualified education programs. Which of those fits depends on how far off the first tuition bill is and on what is already set aside.

What this page does not say

The firm’s bio for Tom Craig records that he was licensed to sell securities and insurance products. This site makes no claim about which licenses are current, because the firm does not publish that. Ask the office directly before relying on it.

Any one step gets you on your way.

Describe what you are holding — a notice, a shoebox, a first year of self-employment, a set of books nobody has closed — and the office will tell you which step it is and what the work involves.
