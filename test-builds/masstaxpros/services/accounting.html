Accounting Services.

Step four, and the one everything else stands on. Books that reconcile, statements somebody can read, and a straight answer about what a compilation is.

What is on the list

  • Financial statement preparation
  • Bookkeeping monthly, quarterly or annually, including QuickBooks training
  • Accounting system setup for new businesses
  • Compilation
  • Business tax returns — sales and use, and business property
  • Business and personal financial statement assistance
  • Business entity selection
  • Consulting services
  • Litigation support

Compilation, in the firm’s own words

Based on client information provided and used primarily for internal use only, as no assurance is expressed.The firm’s own description of the service.

That sentence is worth reading twice, because it is the whole boundary. A compilation puts your figures into statement form. It does not test them, and it does not express an opinion on them. If a bank, a bonding company or a buyer has asked for something with an opinion attached, that is a different engagement and a different firm.

Compilation is the only level of financial statement service this practice advertises.

Entity selection, before the first return

Business entity selection is on the list above. It is worth doing early: how a business is organized decides which return it files, when that return is due, and how profit reaches the owner. Changing it later is possible and is rarely free.

Litigation support

Named on the firm’s list. Where an accounting question is in dispute, the work is assembling and explaining what the records actually show.
