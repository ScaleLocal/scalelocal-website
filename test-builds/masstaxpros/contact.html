What a first call covers

Four questions, in this order. None of them takes long.

  1. Which of the four steps this is — income tax, financial, QuickBooks or accounting.
  2. What already exists: last year’s return, a set of books, a notice, or nothing.
  3. Whether anything has a date on it. A notice almost always does.
  4. What the work involves, so nobody commits before that is clear.

If you are calling about a notice

Have the notice in front of you. The notice number, the tax year and the response date printed on it decide almost everything about what happens next. More on representation.
