Our Story

More Than a Salon. A Sanctuary.

Founded in 2014, Velvet Rose was born from a simple belief: every person deserves to feel extraordinary. Our Beverly Hills studio blends European technique with California warmth, creating an experience that nourishes your spirit as much as your appearance.

From the moment you step through our doors, the outside world disappears. Soft lighting, curated music, the gentle scent of rose and lavender -- every detail is intentional, every touch is artful.

Award-Winning Team

Our artists have been featured in Vogue, Elle, and Harper's Bazaar.

Premium Products Only

We partner exclusively with Oribe, Kerastase, SkinCeuticals, and OPI.

Flexible Scheduling

Early morning to late evening appointments. Because beauty has no curfew.
