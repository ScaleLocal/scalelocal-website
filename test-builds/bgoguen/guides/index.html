The two things people ask before they choose an accountant.

Two questions people ask before they hire anyone

Guides

The same two questions come up before almost every new engagement, and both of them get asked quietly, as though they were obvious. They are not. The first is practical — what do I actually need to bring you. The second is about who is on the other side of the desk — what do the letters after an accountant’s name mean, and does it matter which ones they hold.

Both deserve a real answer rather than a brochure sentence, so each has its own page. Read them before you call anyone, including us.

The guides

What to bring to a tax appointmentThe documents the firm asks for, in roughly the order they arrive — income, deductions, payments already made, and the records a business or rental adds on top. Written to head off the second and third round of questions.Individuals & businesses CPA, EA, MST — what the letters meanWho grants each credential, what it takes to earn and keep, and what it lets the holder do for you. Includes the one thing only a CPA can do, and why representation rights are the distinction that matters most.Credentials explained

Neither guide is a substitute for advice on your own return. They are the background — the part that is the same for everyone.
