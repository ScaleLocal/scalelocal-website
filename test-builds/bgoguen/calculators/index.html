Four calculators

Enough to size a question before you raise it, and no more. They run entirely in your browser — no sign-in, no third-party script, and nothing you enter leaves the page.

Self-employment tax estimateSocial Security and Medicare on net self-employment earnings, with the deductible half broken out.What my retirement account will be worthProjected balance from what you have now plus what you and your employer add each year.Equipment purchase tax savingWhat a Section 179 or bonus-depreciation deduction is worth against your bracket, and the real after-tax cost of the asset.Break-even pointThe revenue and unit volume at which the business stops losing money.

Estimates, not advice

Rates, thresholds and contribution limits change every year, and none of these account for your filing status, your Massachusetts position or anything else on your return. Use one to size a question, then ask about the answer.
