The secure client portal

For existing clients

Clients of the firm have a secure online portal. It is where you send us documents, where you sign what has to be signed before a return can be filed, where messages about your file stay attached to your file, and where an invoice can be reviewed and paid. It is the intended route for anything sensitive going in either direction between you and this office.

Why not just email it

Ordinary email is not a private channel. A message and its attachments pass through servers you do not control, sit in mailboxes on devices that get lost, and stay searchable for years in both your sent folder and ours. A W-2 is a compact package of your name, address, employer and Social Security number — exactly what someone would need to file a fraudulent return under your name before you file your real one.

So: no Social Security numbers, no bank account or routing numbers, no images of driver’s licences, and no brokerage or payroll statements in an unencrypted email. That applies to text in the body of the message as much as to attachments, and it applies to text messages too. If you have already sent something that way, say so — it is worth knowing about, and it is not a reason to avoid mentioning it.

Email is fine for the ordinary questions: scheduling, scope, what a notice means, whether something is deductible in principle.

If you have never used it

Access is set up by the office against the email address we hold for you, so there is nothing to sign up for here. If you have not been invited yet, or the invitation went to an address you no longer use, or you simply cannot get in, call (978) 667-4595 or write to office@bgoguen.com and ask. Say who you are and which email address you would like access to run through. Do not attach anything to that message.
