Five people, named

Billerica, Massachusetts

At Fitzpatrick & Goguen CPAs P.C. you can see who will do your work before you hire anyone. There are five people here. Each one has a page with their credentials, their schooling and how long they have been at the firm, and none of it is written to impress you — it is written so you can check it.

Thomas L. Fitzpatrick IV, MBA, EA, CPA is President & Shareholder. Brian D. Goguen, MST, CPA is a Certified Public Accountant with the firm. Dana Reardon and Sean Malone are accountants, both of whom joined in 2000. Monirina Kim handles firm administration. That is the whole roster, and the work is done by the people on it.

The firm serves clients across the Merrimack Valley and beyond, and describes itself as a family business.

What the letters actually mean

Credentials after a name are not decoration, and they are not interchangeable. Two of the five here hold licenses and credentials that carry specific rights, so it is worth setting out plainly what each one is.

EA — Enrolled Agent

A federal credential granted by the IRS, earned either by examination or by former employment with the IRS. An Enrolled Agent holds unlimited rights to represent taxpayers before the IRS. Thomas earned his in 2017.

CPA — Certified Public Accountant

A license issued by a state board of accountancy. A CPA also holds unlimited representation rights before the IRS, and additionally may perform attest work. Brian has been licensed since 1981; Thomas became a Massachusetts licensed CPA in May 2022.

MST and MBA

An MST is a master’s degree in taxation — a specialist graduate qualification in tax itself, not general accounting. Brian holds one from Bentley College. An MBA is a general master’s in business administration; Thomas earned his at Bowling Green State University.

A preparer without one of these credentials does not hold unlimited representation rights. If you want the distinction explained at more length, see the guide on CPAs and Enrolled Agents.

The same people, year after year

Continuity

In much of this profession the person who knows your file is gone within a couple of years, and the next return starts from a folder rather than from a memory. That is not the case here. Dana Reardon joined in 2000 after working in the mutual fund industry. Sean Malone joined in 2000 as well, straight out of UMass Lowell. Both are still at the desk.

Continuity is worth something concrete at tax time: carryforwards, basis, depreciation schedules, the reason a particular election was made four years ago. Someone who was there when the choice was made does not have to reconstruct it.

Proud members of

The firm belongs to three professional bodies. Each one is a different thing, and membership in each says something different.

American Institute of Certified Public AccountantsThe national professional body for CPAs in the United States, which sets professional and ethical standards for the profession.AICPA
National Association of Enrolled AgentsThe national body for Enrolled Agents, the federally credentialed tax practitioners licensed by the IRS.NAEA
