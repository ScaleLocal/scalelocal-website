What firm administration actually does

Administration in an accounting firm is not a layer between a client and the work. It is the part of the work that keeps everything else in order, and in a five-person office it is visible rather than hidden.

Why the signature step matters

E-file

One piece of administration has a rule behind it rather than a preference. Under IRS e-file rules the taxpayer must sign the authorization before an electronic return originator may transmit the return. A finished, reviewed, entirely correct return sits still until that signature comes back, and the paid preparer must also sign it and give the taxpayer a complete copy.

That is why the chasing is not a formality. A return waiting on a signature in early April is the single most avoidable reason a filing slips, and the follow-up that prevents it is administrative work.

Deadlines do not move for missing paperwork. A federal extension shifts filing to 15 October, and it does not extend the time to pay.
