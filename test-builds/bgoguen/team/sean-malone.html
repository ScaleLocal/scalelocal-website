Accountant

Sean Malone

Sean Malone joined the firm in 2000, the year he graduated from UMass Lowell with a degree in accounting. He has been at 164 Concord Road in Billerica since. There is no earlier employer to list and no later one either — the whole of his working life in accounting has been spent at this one practice, with these colleagues, and in many cases with the same clients.

His title is accountant. He holds no professional credential, and the firm does not claim one for him.

One firm, one career

Staying is easy to overlook, so it is worth being plain about what it produces. An accountant who has worked in one office for a quarter of a century has watched the same client files move through good years and bad ones. He knows how a particular set of books is kept because he has been reading them since they were opened. He knows what was decided in an earlier year, and why, without having to reconstruct it from a scanned copy.

The other half of it is method. Everything Sean knows about preparing a return he learned inside this practice, from the people who still work here. The way a file is assembled, checked and closed is consistent across the accounting staff because it was taught here rather than imported from four previous employers.

Dana Reardon joined the firm the same year.

The ground he works on

Massachusetts

Most of the firm’s work is Massachusetts work, and Massachusetts is not a state where a federal return can simply be copied across.

No state standard deductionMassachusetts taxes most income at a flat 5%, with short-term capital gains at a higher separate rate, and offers no standard deduction. The exemptions and deductions that exist have to be found item by item.Form 1
Residency drives the formResidents file Form 1. Part-year residents and non-residents file Form 1-NR/PY, and a move mid-year changes what belongs on which return.Form 1-NR/PY
Records first, return secondA return is only as sound as the bookkeeping behind it. Where the firm keeps the books, the two pieces of work are done by people who sit in the same office.Bookkeeping
