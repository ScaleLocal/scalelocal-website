Published services

What Dorfman & Dorfman, CPAs publishes

The firm publishes six services. They are reproduced here in the firm’s own words, without addition:

  • “Review and Compilation of small business financial statements”
  • “Bookkeeping Services to trial balance”
  • “Tax Returns including individuals and trusts”
  • “Corporate Tax Returns”
  • “Tax Returns for partnerships and limited liability companies”
  • “Payroll Tax Returns, Federal and State”

Those six lines are grouped below into four pages, one page per kind of engagement.

Scope as written

Three lines that are deliberately narrow

Two of the six services carry a limit inside the sentence itself, and a third names a category that is often assumed to be excluded. Read them literally.

“Bookkeeping Services to trial balance”Ends at trial balance

Not offered

The firm does not perform audits

Financial statement work runs at four levels, in ascending order of work and cost. Preparation assembles statements from the client’s records, with no report and no assurance. Compilation adds a reading for obvious problems and a signed report, still with no assurance. Review applies analytical procedures and enquiry and carries limited assurance. An audit tests, confirms and gathers evidence, and ends in an opinion.

The firm advertises the middle two. An audit is a different engagement and is not among the six published services. There is a second reason the distinction matters: independence is required for assurance work, and an accountant who keeps the books ordinarily cannot also provide assurance on them. A client who needs both bookkeeping and a review should say so early, so the sequence can be settled before the year closes.

If a lender or a contract requires an audited statement, that requirement should be checked in writing before any engagement here is discussed.
