Four calculators.

Enough to size a question before you raise it. They run in your browser, and nothing you enter leaves the page.

The four
01Self-employment tax estimateSocial Security and Medicare on net self-employment earnings, with the deductible half broken out.02Break-even pointThe revenue and unit volume at which the business stops losing money.03Loan payment and payoffPayment, total interest, and what an extra monthly amount does to the payoff date.04Equipment purchase tax savingWhat a Section 179 or bonus-depreciation deduction is worth against your bracket, and the real after-tax cost of the asset.
Estimates, not advice

Rates, thresholds and contribution limits change every year, and none of these account for your filing status, your Massachusetts position or anything else on your return. Use one to size a question, then ask about the answer.
