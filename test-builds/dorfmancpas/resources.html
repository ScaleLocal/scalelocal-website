Five places worth bookmarking.

The primary sources, linked directly. No login, no intermediary, and no version of the rules but the agency’s own.

Where to look
01Internal Revenue ServiceForms, instructions, payment options and refund status.02EFTPS: Electronic Federal Tax Payment SystemThe Treasury system for paying federal business and individual taxes. Enrollment takes several days, so do it before a deposit is due.03Massachusetts Department of RevenueState returns, MassTaxConnect, and the current state filing thresholds.04Massachusetts Attorney GeneralWage and hour rules, and the state’s guidance for employers.05Massachusetts Secretary of the CommonwealthCorporate filings, annual reports and entity lookups.
A word on the links

Five places, not fifty. Each one is the primary source for something a small business or an individual return actually turns on, and each opens in a new tab. If a page has moved since you last looked, say so and it will be corrected.

Before you rely on any of it

Published guidance answers the general question. It does not know your filing status, your entity, or what is already on your books. Call (781) 780-7069 ext. 11 or arrange a time before you act on something you read.
