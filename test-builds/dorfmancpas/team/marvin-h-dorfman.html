Marvin H. Dorfman, CPA

The firm describes itself in one sentence: “Dorfman & Dorfman, CPAs is a small accounting firm owned by Marvin H. Dorfman, CPA and Estee C. Dorfman, CPA, MSA.” Marvin H. Dorfman studied at Bentley College and has practised under three different arrangements.

What follows is only what the dates support. He was senior partner at Dorfman & Goldstein, CPAs until 2004, then practised on his own for four years. In 2008 he formed Dorfman & Dorfman, CPAs with Estee C. Dorfman, who co-founded it. No specialism, client list or anecdote is published, and none is invented here.

Why it matters

The person who takes the call has done all three

Firm size is usually presented as a matter of taste. It is closer to a question of who reads the file. In a large practice, the name on the engagement letter and the person who reviews the depreciation schedule are often not the same. In a two-person firm they are.

Marvin H. Dorfman has worked both ways and chose the smaller twice — alone in 2004, and again in 2008. A client here is not routed to a staff preparer, because there is no staff preparer.

What that costs

A two-person firm has a ceiling. The firm advertises review and compilation of small business financial statements, and bookkeeping to trial balance rather than a full outsourced accounting department. Where an engagement needs more than two accountants, it needs a different firm.
