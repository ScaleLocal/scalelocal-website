Why TNJ asphalt holds up

Edges, base, and grade. Get those right and the rest is gravy.

Most failed driveways failed at the edge first — not enough thickness on the perimeter, no edge restraint, no support against settling. We pour deeper edges and machine-finish them straight. Combined with proper base prep and a real 2% pitch for water, you get a driveway that drains, doesn't crumble, and keeps the front of the house looking sharp.

  • Removed and disposed properly — no piles left at the curb for the homeowner to deal with
  • Compacted gravel base, regraded for proper pitch away from the house
  • Two-lift application: 2" binder course + 1.5" finish course on standard residential
  • Hot-mix delivered direct from the plant — temperature monitored on every load
  • Hand-edged, machine-rolled, and finished clean — no overspray on the lawn or steps
