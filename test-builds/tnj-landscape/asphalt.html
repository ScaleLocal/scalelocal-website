Why our asphalt holds up

Edges, base, and grade — get those right and the rest follows.

Most driveways fail at the edge first — insufficient perimeter thickness, no edge support, settling at the boundaries. We pour deeper edges and machine-finish them clean. Combined with proper base preparation and a true two percent pitch, the driveway drains correctly, holds its shape, and continues to present well from the street.

  • Existing material removed and disposed of completely — no curb piles left behind.
  • Compacted gravel base, regraded to proper pitch away from the structure.
  • Two-lift application: two-inch binder course plus one-and-a-half-inch finish course on standard residential.
  • Hot-mix delivered direct from the plant, temperature monitored on every load.
  • Hand-edged, machine-rolled, and finished clean — no overspray on lawn, steps, or hardscape.
