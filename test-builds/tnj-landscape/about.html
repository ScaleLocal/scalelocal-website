About TNJ

Over a decade.
Same crew, same standards.

TNJ Landscape Masonry & Asphalt is a Wakefield-based residential construction company serving homeowners across Massachusetts and New Hampshire. We focus on three disciplines — masonry and hardscape, asphalt and driveways, and landscape construction — built to a single standard, by the same in-house crew, on every project.

What sets TNJ apart is consistency. Owner Thomas Shidlow walks every property himself before quoting, and he is personally accountable for client satisfaction at every stage. The same in-house crew handles each project from first call to final walkthrough — and that continuity is what protects the quality of the work.

TNJ takes on a measured number of projects each season so every property gets the attention it deserves. A live receptionist or Thomas will take your call during business hours, and Thomas is reachable directly by text for active projects.

Every project gets a personalized proposal — clear scope, clear timeline, fully insured. That has been the standard since day one.

How a project goes

Five steps. No surprises.

Every TNJ project follows the same path from first call to final walkthrough. You'll know who's coming, when, and what they're doing — at every step.

01

Free site visit

Thomas comes out, walks the property, listens to what you want, and measures what's actually buildable.

02

Personalized proposal

You get a written proposal tailored to your project — scope, materials, timeline. No template. No pressure.

03

Schedule the work

We pick a start date that works for your season and your life. You'll know when materials arrive and when the crew shows up.

04

Build it right

Same crew, owner on site. Daily cleanup. We work through your project start-to-finish — no rotating between multiple sites at the same time.

05

Final walkthrough

We walk the finished work with you. You're happy or we keep going. That's the only end state.
