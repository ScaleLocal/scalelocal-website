About TNJ

Twenty years.
Same crew. Same standards.

TNJ is Thomas Shidlow's shop. We work out of Wakefield and stick close to home — five towns, all residential, focused on masonry, asphalt, and the kind of landscape construction that requires real equipment and a real plan.

Over the years we've grown into the heavier trades. We don't chase the mow-and-blow market. We build patios, driveways, walls, and outdoor spaces that have to stand up to twenty New England winters — and the standards are high because Thomas's name and reputation are on every job.

What sets TNJ apart isn't a slogan. Thomas walks every property himself. He's on every job site. He picks up the phone when you call. The same small crew has been building patios and driveways across these five towns for years — that consistency is the whole product.

We deliberately stay focused on five towns. We turn down work outside the radius. That choice means the jobs we say yes to get the attention they're paying for — and means Thomas can be at your property in twenty minutes if a question comes up after the estimate.

No deposit until the day we break ground. Earn it before we ask for it. Everything in writing. Fully insured. That's the standard. Always has been.
