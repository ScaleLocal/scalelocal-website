Asphalt

Asphalt repair and patching done right — when a full repave is overkill.

Pothole repair, cut-and-patch, edge rebuilds. The right call when the rest of the driveway has plenty of life left.

When repair makes sense

Isolated potholes, single-area edge crumbling, frost heave at one section, settled patch from a utility cut. If the failure is local and the rest of the surface is sound, a proper cut-and-patch repair is 80% cheaper than a repave and lasts 10+ years.

Cut-and-patch vs. throw-and-go

The fast/cheap version is throwing cold-patch into a pothole and calling it a day — fails in one winter. The right version is sawcut a clean rectangle, dig out the failed material, recompact base, install hot-mix asphalt in proper lifts, and seam the patch to existing pavement. That's what we do.
