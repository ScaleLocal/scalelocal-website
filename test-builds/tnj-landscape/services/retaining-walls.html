Masonry

Retaining walls engineered for the slope, not just the look.

Segmental block, natural stone, and engineered walls. Properly drained, properly battered, properly stepped. Walls that don't bulge or fail.

Wall types

Segmental block (Versa-Lok, Allan Block, Cornerstone) for clean modern looks and engineered load. Natural stone (fieldstone, granite, ledgestone) for character on older properties. Boulder walls for larger grade changes and rugged aesthetics. Each system has different drainage and base requirements — we'll spec what fits.

Drainage is what saves the wall

Retaining walls fail because of water pressure behind them, not because of the stones in front. Every TNJ wall gets a properly graded gravel chimney, perforated drain pipe daylighted to a positive outlet, and geotextile separation from native soil. The drainage you can't see is the drainage that keeps the wall standing.

Walls under 4 feet vs. taller

Walls under 4 feet (measured from base of footing) typically don't require an engineer's stamp in our area. Anything taller, anything supporting a structure, anything with a slope above it — we recommend engineered design. We'll tell you on site which category your wall falls into.
