Our approach

Over a decade of work. The same crew. The same standards.

TNJ has built outdoor spaces across Massachusetts and New Hampshire for over a decade. The work is exclusively residential, and the standards stay high because every project is owner-led — owner Thomas Shidlow walks each property himself before quoting and is personally accountable for the result.

That accountability is the foundation of how TNJ works. The same in-house crew sees every project from first dig to final walkthrough, and most new work comes from neighbors of homeowners we built for the season before.

How we work

How we run a job site.

Six things you'll notice on our jobs — and keep noticing for years to come.

Owner-led, fully insured

General liability and workers' comp on every job. Thomas is on every site — your property is in the hands of the person whose name is on the company.

Three disciplines, one crew

Masonry, asphalt, and landscape construction handled in-house, by the same team. The transitions between work areas line up because the same hands build all of them.

Built for New England

Years of regional experience inform every decision — proper base depth, frost-aware drainage, and material choices matched to the climate.

Direct line to the owner

Questions, weather calls, schedule updates — Thomas or his receptionist will take your call, and Thomas is just a text away.

Carefully planned

Every project is scoped on site and thought through before the first dig — materials, sequence, and timeline mapped to the property and the season.

Trusted across MA + NH

Wakefield-based, serving homeowners across Massachusetts and New Hampshire — taking on a measured number of projects each season so every one gets the attention it deserves.
