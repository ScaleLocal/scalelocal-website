How we build a patio

What's underneath is what lasts.

Most patios that fail in three years didn't have a surface problem — they had a base problem nine inches under the ground. Compaction, depth, drainage. We get the base right because that's what determines whether the visible work holds up over time.

  • 6"–12" of compacted process gravel base, scaled to the soil and frost depth on your specific lot.
  • Geotextile fabric between subgrade and base when the soil calls for it.
  • Plate-compacted in 2"–3" lifts — not dumped in and rolled over once.
  • Edge restraint mechanically anchored — none of the plastic edging that pops out by year two.
  • Polymeric joint sand activated properly, then sealed once it's cured. Done right.
