The standard we install to

What's beneath the surface decides the lifespan.

Most patios that fail prematurely failed at the base — not the surface. Proper compaction, adequate depth, and considered drainage are the difference between a patio that lasts five years and one that lasts twenty-five. We invest in the foundation work because it determines everything that follows.

  • Six to twelve inches of compacted process gravel base, scaled to substrate and frost depth.
  • Geotextile fabric between subgrade and base on soils that warrant it.
  • Plate-compacted in two- to three-inch lifts for true density.
  • Edge restraint mechanically anchored — no plastic edging that fails in two seasons.
  • Polymeric joint sand, properly activated and sealed once cured.
