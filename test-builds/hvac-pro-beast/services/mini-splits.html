Mini-Splits

Ductless heating and cooling for the spaces ductwork cannot reach — installed and serviced by a specialist.

The right tool for awkward spaces.

Back offices, server closets, additions, garages turned workspaces — mini-splits heat and cool the spots a central system never reaches. "Mini-Split Specialist" is printed on the HVAC Pro Beast business card because it is a core specialty, not a side line.

Mini-split services

  • System sizing and placement for real-world commercial spaces
  • Single-zone and multi-zone installs
  • Service and repairs on existing systems
  • Cleaning and seasonal maintenance

Efficient, quiet, and fast to install.

No ductwork means less disruption and lower install cost — and modern systems handle New England winters. If you have a space that is never the right temperature, a mini-split is usually the answer.
