Ice Machines

When the ice stops, the bar stops. Service, cleaning, and replacement for commercial ice machines across Greater Boston.

No ice is an emergency. We treat it like one.

Ice machines fail on Friday nights, in heat waves, mid-service. HVAC Pro Beast answers 24/7 and gets your machine making ice again — or gets a replacement spec'd and installed if the machine is done.

Ice machine services

  • Diagnosis and repair — no-ice, low-production, and quality issues
  • Cleaning and descaling for consistent, clean ice
  • Water filtration checks and supply-line issues
  • Compressor, pump, and control repairs
  • Replacement and new installs sized to your volume

Maintenance that pays for itself.

Most dead ice machines die of neglect — scale, dirty coils, starved water lines. A simple maintenance schedule keeps production up and rescue calls down.
