Walk-In Coolers & Freezers

Your walk-in holds your inventory. We keep it holding temperature — and answer at 2 AM when it does not.

A warm walk-in is money melting.

Every degree above safe temperature is product at risk. When a walk-in starts climbing, HVAC Pro Beast treats it as the emergency it is — 24/7, with a licensed refrigeration technician on the line, not an answering service.

Walk-in services

  • Emergency no-cool response, day or night
  • Compressor and condenser repairs and replacement
  • Evaporator coils, fan motors, and defrost systems
  • Door gaskets, hinges, and panel issues that leak cold and burn electricity
  • Controls and monitoring, new walk-in installs and box swaps

Catch it before it fails.

Preventive maintenance on a walk-in is cheap insurance: coils cleaned, refrigerant charge checked, gaskets inspected, drains cleared. Businesses on our maintenance schedule rarely meet us at 2 AM.
