Full-Service Roofing

Every Roof. Every Property Type.

From a single skylight to a full commercial flat roof, Ranch Roofing handles the complete range of work for homeowners, condo associations, and businesses across eastern Massachusetts.

Residential Roofing

From everyday repairs to full roof replacements, our team delivers durable, beautifully finished roofs with the kind of care that turns customers into neighbors for life. Asphalt shingle, cedar shake, slate, and metal — backed by GAF Master Elite workmanship coverage up to 25 years.

Condominium Roofing

Commercial Roofing

For businesses large and small, we provide high-quality roofing systems backed by honest guidance and service you can trust from start to finish. EPDM, TPO, PVC, built-up, and modified bitumen — selected for the right building and the right budget, never an upsell.

Commercial Estimate

Roof Deck Replacement

When the decking under your shingles is compromised — from rot, moisture, ice damming, or age — we replace it before installing the new roof. A sound deck is the difference between a 10-year and a 30-year roof, and it's not something to skip.

Schedule Inspection

Skylights

Enhance your home with expertly installed and reliably serviced skylights that bring natural light indoors while maintaining the durability, weather protection, and attention to detail you expect. New installations, replacements, and repairs of leaking or fogged units.

Roof Cleaning & Repair

Not every roof needs a full replacement. Targeted repairs, flashing work, leak diagnostics, gutter cleaning, and soft-wash moss and algae removal — the maintenance work that extends the life of the roof you have. Honest guidance on what your roof actually needs.
