Our Story

It started in 1996, and it hasn't changed much since.

Bob O'Sullivan founded Ranch Roofing in Arlington in 1996 with a simple approach: be honest, be prepared, and do things the right way. Three decades later, that approach is still what defines us.

Over the years we've grown from a single truck to a full crew, expanded from residential into condo associations and commercial flat-roofing, and earned both the GAF Master Elite and Owens Corning Platinum certifications — with GAF Master Elite reserved for fewer than 2% of roofing contractors nationwide.

What hasn't changed: when you call, you're dealing directly with Bob. He stays personally involved in every project. And the job still isn't done until you're 100% satisfied.

"We never cut corners. We don't disappear after install. We've built this company one roof at a time, and we plan to keep it that way."

— Bob O'Sullivan, Owner

Our Values

The three things we've built this company on for the last 30 years.

Certifications & Warranties

Backed By the Industry's Best

The certifications below aren't decoration — they're how we deliver the longest material warranties available in residential roofing.

GAF Master Elite® Certified

A designation GAF reserves for fewer than 2% of roofing contractors nationwide. Provides up to 25 years of workmanship coverage backed directly by GAF. We handle the registration so your coverage starts the day your roof is complete.

Owens Corning® Platinum Preferred

Owens Corning offers coverage for up to 50 years on systems installed by Platinum Preferred Contractors — the longest residential roofing warranty on the market. Available for every Owens Corning system we install.
