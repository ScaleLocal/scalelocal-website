What We Do

Our Masonry Services

Patios, walkways, chimneys, steps, walls, restoration, and tile work — residential masonry in Tewksbury, MA and the surrounding towns.

Full-Service Masonry

Built Right. Built Once.

From a single front-step rebuild to a complete patio install, Reed Masonry handles the entire range of residential masonry and tile work across the Merrimack Valley.

Patios & Pavers

Custom-designed outdoor patios in brick, bluestone, granite, and concrete pavers. We excavate, install a properly graded gravel and stone-dust base, and lay every paver by hand — so your patio stays flat through frost cycles, summer storms, and decades of use.

Request Patio Estimate

Walkways & Driveways

Front walks, side paths, and driveway aprons in stone, brick, and concrete. We rebuild settled or cracked walkways and design new ones to match your home's style. Every walkway is built on a deep base so it stays level — not just for one season, but for the long run.

Get Walkway Quote

Steps & Stairs

Front entry steps, side stairs, and patio steps in granite, bluestone, brick, or concrete. We rebuild crumbling or out-of-code steps with proper risers and treads — safe, code-compliant, and built to match the rest of your home. Handrails optional and welcomed.

Discuss Your Steps

Chimneys

Chimney rebuilds, repointing, crown repair, and cap installation. We work on everything from 1900s brick stacks to modern masonry chimneys — matching mortar joints and brick color so the repair blends seamlessly with the original work. Safety first; we set up proper staging on every job.

Chimney Inspection

Retaining & Garden Walls

Retaining walls, garden walls, and free-standing landscape walls in fieldstone, block, brick, or segmental concrete. We engineer the base and drainage correctly so your wall holds back the slope without bulging, leaning, or failing — and looks good doing it for decades.

Wall Project Quote

Restoration & Tile

Bringing old masonry back to life — repointing brick foundations, repairing failing mortar joints, replacing damaged stones, and restoring historic detail. Plus full tile work for kitchens, bathrooms, and entryways. The careful, slow work of getting it right the first time.

Restoration Visit
