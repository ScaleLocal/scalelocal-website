The Opus Story

Rick founded Opus Plumbing and Heating after more than 20 years working in the plumbing and heating industry. With deep expertise and a passion for exceptional service, he established Opus as an owner-operated business dedicated to serving homeowners throughout Billerica and the surrounding Massachusetts area.

Unlike large corporate chains, Opus remains a hands-on, locally-focused operation where Rick personally oversees every project. We believe in building lasting relationships with our customers based on trust, transparency, and quality work.

"Our commitment is simple: deliver professional, reliable plumbing and heating solutions that exceed your expectations. Every job matters, and we treat your home like our own."

Whether you need a routine maintenance appointment, an emergency repair, or a complete system replacement, you can count on Opus to get the job done right the first time.
