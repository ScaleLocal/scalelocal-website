About Us

Family-Owned. Locally Operated. Built on Trust.

Patriot Plumbing & Drain was founded in 2009 by Mike Sullivan, a Tewksbury native who grew up working alongside his father in the trades. After earning his Master Plumber license and spending years working for large plumbing companies, Mike started Patriot Plumbing with a simple idea: treat every customer's home like his own.

Today we're a team of 6 licensed plumbers serving Tewksbury and the surrounding Merrimack Valley communities. We don't do high-pressure sales. We show up on time, explain what we find, give you an honest price, and do the work right.
