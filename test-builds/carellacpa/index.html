Tax and accounting for people and the businesses they run.

A Certified Public Accountant’s office at 330 Boston Road in North Billerica. Tax preparation and planning, accounting and bookkeeping, financial statement preparation, and business consulting — for individuals and for small businesses.

Where to start

Most people arrive because something happened.

A letter came. A year was never filed. A lender asked for statements. The first 1099 turned up and nobody withheld anything. Start with whichever of these is closest to your situation — each one sets out what is actually involved before you pick up the phone.

A letter arrived from the IRS or DORMost notices are automated and many are simply wrong. The part that matters is the date printed on it.→There are years that were never filedMore common than people assume, and the only version of it that gets worse is the one left alone.→Income is arriving with no tax withheldA first 1099, a side business, a K-1. Nobody is withholding, so the quarterly obligation is now yours.→A business is starting, or changing shapeHow it is organised decides which return it files, when it is due, and how the profit reaches you.→Someone has asked for financial statementsA lender, a landlord or a bonding company. Which level of service they actually need is worth establishing first.→A move into or out of MassachusettsPart-year and non-resident apportionment is one of the more common reasons a return has to be amended.→
The work

Four services, and they are the same conversation.

For somebody who owns a small business, the return, the books, the statements a lender asks for and the decision about next year are not separate problems. They are one problem looked at from four directions, which is the argument for handling them in one place.

Tools

Eight calculators that run on this page.

Mortgage and refinance, loans, retirement and college saving, self-employment tax, equipment purchases and break-even. No third-party script, no sign-in, nothing stored — the arithmetic happens in your browser and stops there.

Monthly mortgage paymentPrincipal, interest, taxes and insurance on a fixed-rate loan, plus the total interest over the life of the note.→Refinance break-evenHow many months of lower payments it takes to earn back what the refinance costs you.→Loan payment and payoffPayment, total interest, and what an extra monthly amount does to the payoff date.→What my retirement account will be worthProjected balance from what you have now plus what you and your employer add each year.→Self-employment tax estimateSocial Security and Medicare on net self-employment earnings, with the deductible half broken out.→Equipment purchase tax savingWhat a Section 179 or bonus-depreciation deduction is worth against your bracket, and the real after-tax cost of the asset.→Break-even pointThe revenue and unit volume at which the business stops losing money.→Saving for collegeWhat a four-year education is projected to cost when your child starts, and what you need to put aside monthly.→
