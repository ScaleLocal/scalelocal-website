The work

Four services, and they are the same conversation.

For somebody who owns a small business, the return, the books, the statements a lender asks for and the decision about next year are not separate problems. They are one problem looked at from four directions, which is the argument for handling them in one place.

Where to start

Most people arrive because something happened.

A letter came. A year was never filed. A lender asked for statements. The first 1099 turned up and nobody withheld anything. Start with whichever of these is closest to your situation — each one sets out what is actually involved before you pick up the phone.

A letter arrived from the IRS or DORMost notices are automated and many are simply wrong. The part that matters is the date printed on it.→There are years that were never filedMore common than people assume, and the only version of it that gets worse is the one left alone.→Income is arriving with no tax withheldA first 1099, a side business, a K-1. Nobody is withholding, so the quarterly obligation is now yours.→A business is starting, or changing shapeHow it is organized decides which return it files, when it is due, and how the profit reaches you.→Someone has asked for financial statementsA lender, a landlord or a bonding company. Which level of service they actually need is worth establishing first.→A move into or out of MassachusettsPart-year and non-resident apportionment is one of the more common reasons a return has to be amended.→

Tools

Seven calculators, run on the page.

A mortgage, a refinance, a loan being paid down early, a retirement projection, the tax on income nobody withheld from, an equipment purchase and a break-even. No borrowed script, no account to open, nothing kept — the sums are worked out on your own machine and go no further.

What the house payment comes toPrincipal, interest and the escrow for tax and insurance on a fixed-rate loan, and what the note costs in interest before it is finished.→When a refinance pays for itselfHow many months of a smaller payment it takes before the cost of doing the refinance has been earned back.→Paying a loan down earlyThe scheduled payment, the interest it carries, and how much sooner the balance clears if you send something extra every month.→Where the retirement account landsA projected balance built from what sits in the account today plus everything you and the employer put in between now and the finish.→Tax on income with nothing withheldSocial Security and Medicare on profit from self-employment, with the half that comes back as a deduction shown separately.→What the equipment really costsWhat writing an asset off is worth at the rate you actually pay, and what the purchase leaves you out of pocket once the deduction is counted.→The point the business stops losingThe volume and the revenue at which a month’s fixed costs are finally covered, and what it takes to clear a profit on top of them.→
