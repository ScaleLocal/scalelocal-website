Cross eighty percent and a lender will normally want mortgage insurance on top of the payment.

Mortgage insurance, association dues and the costs of closing all sit outside this. The escrow line is the two annual figures you typed, divided by twelve. Both of those figures are set again every year, so read the monthly total as this year’s and not as a fixed number.
