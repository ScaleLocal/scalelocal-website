Start where you are.

Most people call an accountant because something specific happened. Whichever of these is closest, the page sets out what is actually involved — the deadlines that matter, the order the work runs in, and what to have ready.

The list
A letter arrived from the IRS or DORMost notices are automated and many are simply wrong. The part that matters is the date printed on it.→There are years that were never filedMore common than people assume, and the only version of it that gets worse is the one left alone.→Income is arriving with no tax withheldA first 1099, a side business, a K-1. Nobody is withholding, so the quarterly obligation is now yours.→A business is starting, or changing shapeHow it is organized decides which return it files, when it is due, and how the profit reaches you.→Someone has asked for financial statementsA lender, a landlord or a bonding company. Which level of service they actually need is worth establishing first.→A move into or out of MassachusettsPart-year and non-resident apportionment is one of the more common reasons a return has to be amended.→
Or by service

If you already know what you need rather than what happened, the four practice areas are described in full.

Tax preparation and planningFederal and Massachusetts returns for individuals and small businesses, and the decisions that have to be made before the year closes.→Accounting and bookkeepingThe monthly discipline underneath everything else: a chart of accounts that fits the business, and accounts that reconcile.→Financial statement preparationStatements a lender, a landlord or an owner can read, and a straight answer about which level of service the request requires.→Business consultingThe questions that arrive between filings: how to organize, what a hire costs, whether to buy the equipment.→

Describe what happened, and ask what it takes to sort out.

There is no fee schedule to quote from, because no two of these are the same. A first call establishes what the work actually involves before anybody commits to it.
