Our Divisions

Complete Exterior Solutions

From flat commercial roofs to residential shingle, gutters and custom metal work to windows and siding — T.G. Lambert Roofing handles every part of your building's exterior. One trusted name, since 1932.

Division 1

Commercial Roofing

Lambert has been a fixture on Merrimack Valley commercial buildings for generations — from light industrial and warehouse to retail, office, and institutional properties. We work with property managers, owners, and GCs on systems sized to your building, budget, and lifecycle.

  • Flat & modified bitumen roofing
  • TPO single-ply systems
  • New installation, replacement & repair
  • Solar systems & solar-ready roofs
  • Preventive maintenance programs
Call (978) 374-9224
Division 2

Residential Roofing

Full roof replacements, repairs, and storm work for single-family homes from Haverhill to Andover. We install premium CertainTeed shingles and use the right underlayment, flashing, and ventilation details to make a roof that lasts.

  • Architectural & specialty shingle systems
  • New installation, replacement & repair
  • Storm damage and insurance work
  • Solar-ready installations
  • Ice & water shield, ridge ventilation, flashing
Call (978) 374-9224
Division 3

Gutters & Custom Metal

Seamless aluminum gutters cut on-site, leaf-guard systems, and custom architectural metal flashing and trim fabricated to your home's exact details. We size, pitch, and hang each run to keep water moving where it belongs.

  • Seamless aluminum gutters — multiple colors
  • Leaf guards & gutter protection
  • Custom flashing, drip edge, and trim
  • New installation, repair, and replacement
  • Architectural copper and accent metal
Call (978) 374-9224
Division 4

Windows & Siding

Lambert is a full exterior contractor — not just roofs. We install energy-efficient replacement windows and durable, low-maintenance siding systems that increase your home's curb appeal, comfort, and value.

  • Energy-efficient replacement windows
  • Vinyl & fiber-cement siding systems
  • Specialty trim & architectural detail
  • New installation, repair, and replacement
  • Low-maintenance materials
