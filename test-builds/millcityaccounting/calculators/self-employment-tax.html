What this does not cover

This is self-employment tax only. It is not your income tax, and it does not account for filing status, the qualified business income deduction, or state tax. Rates and the wage base change annually.

One Massachusetts note

The Social Security portion stops at an annual wage base that is adjusted each year; the Medicare portion does not. This estimates self-employment tax only — income tax sits on top of it.
