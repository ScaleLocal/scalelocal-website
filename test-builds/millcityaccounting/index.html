Why those two

Not a marketing choice

Scott Marchlik spent a decade at an accounting firm in Cambridge before starting Mill City in 2018, and his primary clientele there were quick-serve restaurant owners and rental real estate owners. Those are the two businesses he has done the most of, so they are the two this office is built around.

Everything else — a contractor, a consultancy, a personal return with a rental attached — is welcome and familiar. But if you are one of those two, you are talking to somebody who has already seen your year.
