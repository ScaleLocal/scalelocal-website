Professional Services

Complete Tree Care Services

From hazardous tree removal to plant health care, High Line Tree handles every aspect of professional arborist services.

01

Tree Removal

Safe removal of hazardous, dead, storm-damaged, or unwanted trees. Every removal is completed with precision and includes full clean-up.

  • Hazardous tree assessment
  • Precision felling techniques
  • Complete debris removal
  • Bobby oversees every job
02

Tree Pruning

Expert aesthetic and structural pruning by a Certified Arborist. Improves tree health, appearance, and safety with science-backed techniques.

  • Crown thinning
  • Crown raising
  • Deadwood removal
  • Vista pruning
03

Hazardous Tree Removal

Emergency removal of trees posing immediate danger to people or property. Rapid assessment and safe extraction in critical situations.

  • Storm damage response
  • Leaning tree removal
  • Root failure assessment
  • Hanging limb removal
04

Cabling & Bracing

Structural support systems that extend the life of valuable mature trees with weak branch unions or co-dominant stems.

  • Cable installation
  • Brace systems
  • Union support
  • Tree preservation
05

Plant Health Care

Comprehensive health assessments, diagnosis, and treatment. Bobby's PHC certification ensures proper science-backed care for your landscape.

  • Tree health assessments
  • Soil testing
  • Fertilization programs
  • Pest & disease management
06

New Tree Planting

Expert species selection, site assessment, and professional installation. Bobby guides you to the right tree for the right location.

  • Species selection
  • Site assessment
  • Professional installation
  • Right tree placement
07

Stump Grinding

Complete stump removal below grade level for a clean finish. Ready for reseeding, landscaping, or new planting with thorough debris cleanup.

  • Complete stump removal
  • Below-grade grinding
  • Debris removal
  • Ready for landscaping
08

Storm Damage Response

24/7 emergency response for storm-damaged trees. Rapid assessment, safe removal of fallen trees and debris, and property protection.

  • 24/7 emergency response
  • Storm damage assessment
  • Fallen tree removal
  • Property protection
