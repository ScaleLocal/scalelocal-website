Tree Removal Questions

How much does tree removal cost in Massachusetts?

Tree removal costs depend on tree height, diameter, location on the property, proximity to structures, equipment needed, and cleanup scope. A 30-foot oak near a deck costs more than a 40-foot tree in the middle of a field. Bobby provides free estimates so you know the exact cost before work begins—no surprises.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Tewksbury or surrounding areas?

Permit requirements vary by town. Many Merrimack Valley municipalities don't require permits for hazardous or dead trees on residential property, but some towns have local regulations or size thresholds. Bobby is familiar with local bylaws and can advise whether permits are needed. When permits are required, he handles the paperwork.

How long does tree removal take?

A small-to-medium tree (under 40 feet, clear site) typically takes 2-4 hours. Large trees near structures may take a full day or multiple days. Complex removals with crane operations take longer because safety is the priority. Bobby will give you a realistic timeline during the estimate.

What happens to the wood after removal?

Bobby chips branches and small wood on-site (you can take mulch home) or hauls it away. Larger logs can be left for firewood if you'd like, or removed. Hard-to-handle debris is disposed of responsibly. The stump is either left for your landscape design or ground below grade.

Is tree removal covered by homeowners insurance?

Insurance may cover removal if the tree fell in a storm or poses an immediate hazard. Pre-emptive removal of a dead or diseased tree is rarely covered. Check your policy and call your insurer—Bobby can provide documentation if needed for a claim.
