Andover Tree Care Built on Experience

Andover is home to Phillips Academy and surrounded by the natural beauty of Harold Parker State Forest—a town that values its canopy. The mix of mature oaks, hemlocks, and white pines defines the neighborhood character. These trees also demand respect. A hazardous limb or diseased trunk near your home needs expert hands and honest assessment.

Our work here spans nearly two decades. We've cleared estates, handled post-storm emergency response, and worked on properties around the academy grounds. We know which trees thrive in Andover's soil, which ones face pest pressure (winter moth and emerald ash borer are confirmed concerns), and which actions will keep your property safer and your landscape healthier long-term.

We start every job the same way: a free on-site estimate where we listen to your concerns, evaluate the trees, and explain our approach in plain language. No sales pitch, no pressure. Just the information you need to make the right call.

Andover Tree Questions Answered

What tree removal rules apply in Andover?
+

Andover has bylaws protecting certain trees and species. Properties near Harold Parker State Forest may have additional restrictions. We always research local requirements before starting work and obtain necessary permits. Your free estimate includes a clear explanation of what applies to your specific situation.

I think my oak tree might be dying. What do I do?
+

Call for a professional assessment. We inspect for disease, pest damage, structural problems, and overall health. Sometimes a tree can be saved with proper care; sometimes removal is the safer choice. Early detection matters, so don't wait if you notice decline.

How much will pruning cost for my old hemlock?
+

Pruning cost depends on tree size, condition, height, and branch density. Without seeing it, we can't give a fair price over the phone. A free on-site estimate lets us measure and assess, then provide you a firm quote before work begins.

Are you available for emergency storm cleanup?
+

Yes. We offer 24/7 emergency response for storm damage, fallen trees, and urgent hazards. Call immediately if you have a dangerous situation. We prioritize emergency calls and can often respond same-day in Andover.
