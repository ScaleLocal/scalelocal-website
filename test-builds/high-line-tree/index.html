Complete Tree Care Services

From hazardous tree removal to aesthetic pruning, High Line Tree Service handles every aspect of professional tree care for residential and commercial properties.

01

Tree Removal

Safe, efficient removal of hazardous, storm-damaged, or unwanted trees. Bobby is on-site for every job, ensuring precision from start to finish.

02

Tree Pruning

Expert aesthetic and structural pruning to promote tree health, improve appearance, and eliminate safety hazards. Certified Arborist techniques on every project.

03

Emergency Service

24/7 emergency storm response and hazardous tree removal. When severe weather strikes, High Line Tree is ready to respond immediately.
