Bobby's Journey

From Tewksbury Native to Certified Arborist

Bobby Black started his tree care journey while still pursuing a criminal justice degree at a local college in Massachusetts. Rather than follow the traditional path, he realized his true passion was working with trees and people. He began learning the fundamentals at a local tree care company, immersing himself in both the science and art of arboriculture.

After mastering the technical skills, Bobby pursued his Plant Health Care (PHC) certification and earned his ISA Certified Arborist credential. He then spent six years as a crew leader at The Care of Trees in Chicago, one of the nation's most respected tree care companies, where he refined his expertise and leadership skills on complex projects ranging from hazardous tree removal to aesthetic pruning.

In 2016, Bobby returned home to New England with a vision: to bring that same level of professional excellence back to his community. High Line Tree Service was founded on a simple principle: every client deserves direct access to the owner, and every job deserves the personal attention of a Certified Arborist who truly cares about doing it right.
