About High Line Tree Service

Meet Bobby Black, Certified Arborist and owner. With 18+ years of professional tree care experience, Bobby is committed to delivering expert service on every single job.

Our Story

Bobby's Path to Professional Arboriculture

From Education to Tree Work

Bobby Black started his career with a degree in criminal justice while working for a local tree company. There, he discovered his passion for climbing, pruning, and the technical demands of professional tree care.

Pursuing Professional Certification

Committed to mastering his craft, Bobby studied Plant Health Care extensively and earned his professional Certified Arborist credential through the International Society of Arboriculture. This certification represents rigorous knowledge of tree biology, safety, and best practices in modern arboriculture.

Leadership in Chicago

Bobby joined The Care of Trees in Chicago, one of the nation's leading tree service companies, where he worked as crew leader for 6 years. This experience taught him how to manage complex projects, lead teams, and maintain the highest standards of quality and safety.

Returning to New England

After years in Chicago, Bobby returned to New England and founded High Line Tree Service in Tewksbury, MA. He brought his deep expertise and commitment to personal service, building a reputation for thorough, professional tree care throughout Northeastern Massachusetts.

Owner-Operator Philosophy

High Line Tree stands apart because Bobby is present on every job from start to finish. You don't work with a franchise or a middleman. When you hire High Line Tree, you get the owner conducting your tree assessment, managing your project, and ensuring your complete satisfaction.

A Full-Service Company

Today, High Line Tree offers comprehensive services including tree removal, pruning, cabling and bracing, plant health care, new tree planting, and 24/7 emergency response. All work is fully insured and performed with the professional standards Bobby learned at the nation's best tree companies.
