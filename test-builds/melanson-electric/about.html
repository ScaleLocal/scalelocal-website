Owner Story

Meet Ray Melanson

Ray Melanson started wiring houses in Southeastern Mass over 35 years ago, working through every level of the trade — apprentice, journeyman, then master — and earning his Massachusetts Master Electrician license, number 21494-A, that he carries to every single job.

Today Melanson Electric & Safety (operating publicly as Next Electric Pro) is headquartered at 219 Wilbur Avenue in Somerset and serves 25+ towns across Southeastern Massachusetts and into Rhode Island. The business is still family-owned. Ray still answers the phone. And every job — residential, commercial, industrial — is held to the same standard: permitted, inspected, and signed off.

What's grown over those 35 years isn't the company — it's the reputation. The kind of footprint you can only build by doing every job right, one at a time, for three and a half decades.

"I'm a master electrician. That means the buck stops with me. Every panel I install, every charger, every generator — if it's wrong, I make it right. That's how my dad did it, that's how I do it, and that's the only way I know."
— Raymond D. Melanson, Owner

Ray is an active member of the International Association of Electrical Inspectors (IAEI), holds OSHA 10 certification, and carries license A-003971 in Rhode Island in addition to his Mass Master 21494-A and Construction Supervisor 86225. When something changes in the code book, he reads it before the inspector does.

If you're calling for an estimate, you're calling Ray. If a crew member shows up, you'll meet Ray first. The way it should be.
