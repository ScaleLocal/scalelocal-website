Electrical Work, Done Right

Service Upgrades & Panels

Meet Ray Melanson

Melanson Electric & Safety isn't a franchise, and it isn't a call center. When you call (774) 497-1495, you're talking to the people who'll actually be at your panel.

Ray Melanson holds Massachusetts Master Electrician license 21494-A and Rhode Island license A-003971. He's OSHA 10 certified, a member of the International Association of Electrical Inspectors (IAEI), and has spent 35+ years wiring the homes and businesses of Southeastern Mass — from single-circuit additions to full commercial fit-outs.
