Generator Installation

Storms knock out Southeastern Mass power three or four times every year, sometimes for days. A standby generator means the lights stay on, the sump pump runs, the freezer keeps food, and the heat doesn't die in February. We size it, install it, coordinate the gas hookup, and test the auto-transfer switch top to bottom.

  • Whole-home automatic standby (Generac, Kohler, Briggs & Stratton)
  • Commercial backup power systems
  • Auto-transfer switch installation & testing
  • Natural gas / propane line coordination
  • Critical-circuit sub-panel wiring
  • Permit pull & municipal inspection
