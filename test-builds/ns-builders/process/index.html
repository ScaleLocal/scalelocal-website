01

Initial Contact & Consultation

First Phone Call

We start by listening to your vision, goals, and expectations. We’ll dive deeper into your project and determine if we’re the right fit.

Consultation Meeting

Next, we get to know each other better by reviewing inspiration, sketches, and requirements. We’ll walk through each phase of our process.

Schedule a consultation → Download full process document

02

Pre-Construction

Collaborative Planning & Design

We bring in architects and designers to start developing a plan. Through site visits, studies, and estimating, we’ll outline next steps.

Pre-Construction Communication

You’ll receive access to our client portal, including 24/7 access to necessary information. We’ll schedule regular check-ins and establish clear deadlines for critical decisions.

03

Proposal & Contract

Detailed Project Proposal

We’ll share a scope of work and estimated schedule. Open-book budgeting provides ultimate cost transparency.

Contract Signing

We’ll meet to walk through contract details, ensuring there are no surprises before we begin construction.

04

Construction

Kickoff & Updates

Meet with the on-site team and receive a welcome packet with key contacts and expectations. Receive daily photos, weekly email updates, and more on the status of your project.

Communication & Client Management

Your client portal provides direct access to the project manager, ensuring proactive issue resolution, assistance with material selections, and dedicated support.

05

Project Completion

Walkthrough

Together, we’ll conduct final quality checks before move-in.

Orientation & Move-in

We’ll provide hands-on demonstrations of all home systems. You’ll receive a home-operation guide with warranty information and more.

06

Post-Construction

Warranty and Home Care Program

Scheduled follow-ups ensure everything is to your satisfaction. NS Builders Home Care provides lifetime maintenance.
