The three promises

Every membership comes with the same three. They don't change, they aren't negotiable, and they don't cover whoever you brought with you.

01

You get a shot at every break

Breaks run weekly right now, and that number is going up. Seats are announced, they go live, and it's first come first served. When they're full they're full. Membership is what puts you in the running. The rest is how fast you move.

02

You never open nothing

Every break has a floor under it. A value we guarantee you won't land below. The grails are luck. The floor isn't. And you know the number before you take a seat.

03

Something always turns up

Seats to a game. A card we couldn't bring ourselves to list. First dibs on a high level break. It's never the same twice. And the room knows what it's looking at.
