What waits on the other side

Three standing privileges, unchanged since the house was instituted. They are not advertised elsewhere, and they are not extended to guests.

I

First Refusal

Every consignment of consequence is shown to the membership before it is shown to a market. You see it first, at the number the house paid, or you never see it listed at all.

II

The Allotment

Four times a season, a sealed allocation arrives without invoice — a case break held back, a one-of-one released from a private cabinet. Nothing is asked in return.

III

The Table

One evening each year, in a room that is not named on the invitation. Grading on the spot, trades settled by handshake, and the only guest list that has ever mattered.
