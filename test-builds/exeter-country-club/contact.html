Location & Directions

Driving Directions

From North (I-95 South)

I-95 South → Exit 9 toward Newport/South County (RI-4 South) → Exit 3B toward Exeter (RI-102 North) → merge onto RI-102 N/Victory Hwy. Exeter Country Club is 2.1 miles on the left.

From South (I-95 North)

I-95 North → Exit 3A toward North Kingstown → right onto RI-3 → follow to RI-102 → left at light → 5 miles. Exeter Country Club on right across from fire house.
