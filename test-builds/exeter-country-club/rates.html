Tee Time Information

  • Advance Reservations: Tee times reserved up to 5 days in advance
  • Book Your Tee Time: Contact Pro Shop at (401) 295-8212, in-person or by phone
  • Arrival Time: Arrive 15 minutes before your tee time and check in at Pro Shop
  • Single Players: Arrive 45 minutes early for available openings
  • Payment: All major credit cards accepted, cash preferred
  • Deposits: Credit card deposit required for Friday-Sunday tee times (Visa/MC only)
