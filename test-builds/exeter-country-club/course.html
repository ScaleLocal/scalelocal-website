One of Rhode Island's Finest

Long considered one of Rhode Island's finest championship courses, Exeter Country Club was designed by legendary architect Geoffrey Cornish and opened in 1964. The course winds through the scenic countryside of southern Rhode Island with rolling fairways, strategic bunkering, and elevation changes that reward thoughtful play at every ability level.

Sharpen Your Game

Practice Facilities

Dedicate time to improvement with our complete practice complex featuring natural turf, dedicated practice areas, and ideal conditions for working on every aspect of your game.

01

Grass Driving Range

Natural turf practice range—not mats. Work on your full swing with realistic turf feedback from the same quality grass as the course.

Course Rules

Policies & Dress Code

Proper Golf Attire Required

No cut-offs or tank tops. Collared shirts encouraged for a polished appearance befitting our championship course.

Pace of Play

Groups should maintain a pace of approximately 4.5 hours for 18 holes to ensure enjoyment for all players.

Cart Eligibility

Must be 16+ with a valid driver's license to operate a golf cart. Two players maximum per cart.

Group Size

Maximum four players per group. Groups of 20+ must use mandatory carts for efficient course management.

Course Amenities

No outside alcohol or large coolers permitted. Credit card required for Friday through Sunday tee times (Visa/MC accepted).

Age & Play

Minimum age 8 to play the course. All players under 18 must be accompanied by an adult.

Spectators & Guests

No spectators or ride-along non-players permitted on the course. All players must pay appropriate green fees.

Course Safety

No glass bottles on the course. USGA Rules of Golf apply. Play respectfully and give way to faster groups.

Play By The Rules

Local Course Rules

Roads and paths within property limits are not hazards. Out of bounds: right of hole 4, left of hole 8, and right of and over the fence on hole 12. Out of bounds over walls on holes 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, and 9. Give way to faster players and wave groups forward on par 3 holes to maintain pace of play.
