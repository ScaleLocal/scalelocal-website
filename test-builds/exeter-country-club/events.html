Outings & Events

From corporate tournaments to private celebrations, we handle every detail.

Premium Golf Outings

Professionally managed golf outings for corporate and charity groups, bachelor parties, and birthdays. Our team brings decades of experience to every event, ensuring flawless execution from the first tee to the final toast.

  • Customizable packages to fit your group and budget
  • Full event coordination from tee to table
  • Championship course on a Geoffrey Cornish design
  • Professional tournament management available

Contact Jennifer at (401) 295-1178 to discuss your vision.

Types of Events We Host

01

Corporate Outings

Team building, client entertainment, and networking events on a championship course. Perfect for strengthening relationships and making lasting impressions.

02

Charity Tournaments

Professionally organized fundraiser tournaments with full scoring, leaderboards, and prize selections. We handle all logistics so you can focus on your cause.

03

Private Celebrations

Bachelor parties, milestone birthdays, retirement celebrations, and special occasions. Create unforgettable memories with your closest friends and family.

League Play

Weekday afternoon leagues Monday through Thursday with both competitive and social play options. Join our community of passionate golfers.

The Complete Outing Package

18 Holes Championship Golf

Play our Geoffrey Cornish-designed course, recognized for its challenging layout and pristine conditioning.

Signature Steak Fry Dinner

Steaks prepared to order on our open-fire pit, served on the brick patio with views of the course.

Table Service & Patio Dining

Brick patio overlooking the course with indoor backup for inclement weather. Full beverage and appetizer service.

Beverage Cart Service

On-course refreshments and snacks available throughout your round. Note: Coolers are not permitted; use our beverage cart.

Prize Selection

Choose from our fully stocked Golf Shop for tournament prizes and awards. Professional scoring available.

Electric Golf Carts

Modern, well-maintained carts for comfortable transportation throughout your round.
