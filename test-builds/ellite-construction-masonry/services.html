What We Build

Masonry & Hardscape Services

Stone walls, retaining walls, walkways, patios, stairs, and stone veneer — for homes across Dracut, Lowell, Methuen, Tewksbury, and Tyngsborough.

Full-Service Masonry

Six Core Services. Built to Last.

Whether it's a single stoop or a full backyard transformation, we handle the design, the prep, and the install — and we stay until it's right.

Stone Walls

Rustic or modern stone walls, with the best materials, to bring your home a refined finish. Garden walls, sitting walls, and decorative property-line walls — sized and styled for your yard. We pick stone that ties into the existing look of your house instead of fighting it.

Request Stone Wall Quote

Retaining Walls

For a protective wall you need something strong and imposing — with proper drainage, the right footing, and a clean finished look. We build segmental block, natural stone, and engineered retaining walls that hold a slope and look the part for decades.

Discuss Your Slope

Walkways

Stone walkways for the exterior of your home, set on a properly prepared base for years of durability. Bluestone, brick, paver, or natural irregular stone — with edge restraints and joint detail done so the walkway stays level and clean.

Plan Your Walkway

Patios

Make the most of the space in your yard with a patio built from quality materials for a modern environment outside your home. We lay out the patio for how you'll actually use it — grill area, table, fire pit — and prep the base so it stays flat through New England winters.

Design Your Patio

Stairs

A staircase service that values quality and durability while still offering a modern design. Granite treads, bluestone steps, brick stoops, and concrete — built for safe, even risers and a finished look that lifts the whole entrance to your home.

Get Stair Quote

Stone Veneer

A modern finish for your home with stone veneer detail that brings the sophistication you want. Chimneys, foundations, columns, and accent walls — the look of full stone without the cost, weight, or structural changes a full stone install would need.

Veneer Estimate
How We Work

Our 4-Step Process

A straightforward approach with no surprises and no pressure — from first walk-through to final cleanup.

1

Free On-Site Quote

We come out, walk the project with you, and listen to what you're picturing. We measure, we photograph, and we explain what's involved.

2

Clear Written Estimate

You get a written proposal with materials, scope, and timeline. Plain language, no hidden fees, and no pressure to decide on the spot.

3

Careful Installation

We protect your lawn and driveway, keep the site clean each day, and stay on schedule. The same crew shows up every day until it's done.

4

Final Walk-Through

We walk the finished project with you. Nothing's done until you're happy with it — and we're still local if anything ever needs attention.
