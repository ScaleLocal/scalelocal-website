About Us

A Local Masonry Crew, Built on Quality

Ellite Construction is a residential masonry and hardscape contractor based in Dracut, Massachusetts. We build the outdoor work that makes a home feel finished.

Our Story

Stone, Brick & Hardscape — The Way It Should Be Done

Ellite Construction has been in the residential construction business for years, focused on bringing quality materials and careful craftsmanship to homeowners in the Merrimack Valley.

We started doing masonry because we love the work. Stone and brick aren't quick jobs — they have to be set right, with the proper base, the proper drainage, and the proper detail. When we finish a project, it has to look like it belongs there, and it has to be there for the long run.

That focus on doing it the right way has earned Ellite a reputation as a reliable, expert masonry contractor across Dracut, Lowell, Methuen, Tewksbury, and Tyngsborough — trusted by homeowners who care how their property looks and how long it lasts.
