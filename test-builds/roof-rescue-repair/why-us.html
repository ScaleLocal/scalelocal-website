Four Reasons

What Makes Us Different

The kind of things that matter when a roof problem hits your house at 11pm in February.

Repair-First, Always

Most "you need a new roof" calls are actually a localized failure — flashing, sealant, a small section of shingles. We're set up to diagnose and fix those, not just quote the biggest job we can. That alone saves our customers thousands.

Honest, No-Pressure Inspections

Free, on-site, about 30 minutes. We climb up, take photos, and walk through what we see — including the parts that don't need attention yet. You get a written quote that's plain English, no expiring-discount nonsense, and you're free to think about it.

Specialty Repair Skills

Cedar shake, standing-seam metal, ice-dam steaming, chimney and skylight flashing, Roof Maxx® rejuvenation. The work that general roofers often pass on — that's our daily work. Multiple roof types, multiple problem types.

Local, Owner-Operated

Kate Gagnon runs the business hands-on out of Dover. We're not a franchise, we're not a call center, and we don't disappear after the truck pulls out of your driveway. You'll have the same number to call next year, and the year after.
