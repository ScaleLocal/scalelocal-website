Why Homeowners Call Us First

Three Reasons Neighbors Refer Us

A different kind of roofer for the Seacoast — one that earns the work by saving you money first.

Repair-First Philosophy

Most roof problems — loose shingles, leaking flashing, ice damage, failed sealant — don't need a full tear-off. We find what's actually wrong and fix it, often for a fraction of what a replacement would cost.

Honest, On-Site Inspections

We climb the roof, take photos, and walk you through exactly what we see — including what doesn't need attention yet. No pressure to sign on the spot. No expiring-discount nonsense.

Specialty Repair Skills

From cedar shake to standing-seam metal, leaking skylights to chimney flashing to ice-dam damage — we handle the work that general roofers often pass on. Including Roof Maxx® rejuvenation to extend shingle life by years.

What We Fix

Every Roof Type. Every Common Problem.

Whether it's a few missing shingles or a chimney flashing that's been leaking for years, we've seen it on the Seacoast and we know how to fix it without selling you a whole new roof.

Asphalt Shingle Repair & Replace

Loose, missing, or delaminated shingles. Wind damage. Granule loss. Targeted repairs first — full replacements only when the roof is truly done.

Learn More

Flashing & Leak Repair

Chimneys, skylights, dormers, valleys, roof-to-siding transitions. The vast majority of "I need a new roof" calls turn out to be a flashing issue. We find it, we fix it.

Learn More

Roof Maxx® Rejuvenation

An EPA-safe treatment that restores flexibility to aging asphalt shingles — adding years of life and delaying replacement. Authorized application on the NH Seacoast.

Learn More

Cedar Shake

Natural cedar repair, selective replacement, and full reroofs. We handle the species, the moss, and the fastener choices that make a cedar roof last decades on the Seacoast.

Learn More

Metal Roofing

Standing seam, ribbed panel, and metal shingle systems. Repairs to existing metal roofs — popped fasteners, sealant failures, panel damage — plus full metal-roof installs in multiple colors.

Learn More

Ice Dams & Winter Damage

Steam-removal of dangerous ice dams, repair of the shingles and flashing they damage, plus insulation and ventilation work to keep them from coming back next winter.

Meet Kate Gagnon

Roof Rescue Repair isn't a franchise. It isn't a call center. When you book an inspection, you're talking to the same team that's going to be on your roof.

Kate Gagnon runs Roof Rescue Repair (the operating name of Scout Roof Rescue, LLC) out of Dover, NH, with a clear focus: do the repair the right way the first time, only push for a full replacement when the roof is truly past saving, and stand behind the work after the truck pulls away.
