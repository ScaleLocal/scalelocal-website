Our Story

Built on a Simple Idea: Fix It Before You Replace It.

Roof Rescue Repair (the operating name of Scout Roof Rescue, LLC) was founded in Dover, NH with one clear focus that most local roofers don't share: actually look at the roof, find what's actually wrong, and fix it without selling a tear-off when a tear-off isn't needed.

Kate Gagnon runs the company hands-on. That means when you call to book an inspection, you're talking to the same person who'll be assessing your roof — and standing behind the work afterward.

We work across the NH Seacoast, into Southern Maine, and down through Northern Massachusetts. Asphalt, cedar shake, metal, flat — plus the specialty repair work (flashing, ice dams, skylights, Roof Maxx® rejuvenation) that keeps a roof going for years longer than a full replacement would suggest.

"A lot of roofers will quote you the biggest job they can sell. We'd rather earn your trust on a small repair and have you call us back in ten years."

— Kate Gagnon, Owner

What We Stand For

Our Values

Three things that shape every estimate, every repair, and every replacement.

Honesty

We tell you what your roof actually needs — not what makes the biggest invoice. If a flashing repair will fix the leak, we say so. If the roof really is done, we say that too, with photos and reasons.

Repair-First Skill

Anyone can sell a tear-off. The harder — and usually more useful — work is diagnosing what's actually leaking, what's truly worn out, and what just needs a fix. That's the work we specialize in.

Lifetime Relationships

We're local, owner-operated, and not going anywhere. We'd rather do five repairs over fifteen years for the same family than one big replacement and never see them again. The math works for everyone.
