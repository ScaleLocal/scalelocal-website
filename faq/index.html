FAQ

Questions about ScaleLocal, answered.

Straight answers about what ScaleLocal does, what it costs, the areas we serve, and how web design, SEO, and AI automation work together for local service businesses.

What does ScaleLocal do?

ScaleLocal is a web design, SEO, and AI growth agency based in Tewksbury, Massachusetts. We build local service businesses a modern website, optimize it to get found on Google, Bing, and AI search, and add AI tools that capture every lead. One partner for the part of your business that lives online.

What areas does ScaleLocal serve?

ScaleLocal is based in Tewksbury, MA and serves local service businesses across Massachusetts, New Hampshire, and Rhode Island — including Lowell, Andover, Billerica, Chelmsford, Methuen, Haverhill, Nashua, Manchester, Salem, Providence, and the surrounding towns.

How much does ScaleLocal cost?

ScaleLocal plans range from $249 to $1,497 per month, depending on how much web design, SEO, and AI automation a business needs. Pricing is transparent with no hidden fees. The full breakdown of what is included at each level is on our pricing page.

How fast can ScaleLocal build my website?

ScaleLocal builds a free website demo within 48 hours, so you can see your new site before committing to anything. The full build timeline after that depends on the scope, and we give you a clear timeline up front.

Do I need a new website, or just a redesign?

It depends on the site you have. If the foundation is sound, a redesign modernizes what you have for less. If the site is past saving or you have none, a ground-up website build is the right path. We look at where you stand and tell you honestly which one fits.

Will my website get found on Google?

Getting found is the goal of every ScaleLocal build. We engineer the site for search from the foundation — technical SEO, schema, speed, and local signals — and layer in local SEO so customers searching nearby find you. Search rankings depend on competition and take time, and we set honest expectations rather than promising specific positions.

What is AI search, and why does it matter?

AI search is when customers ask tools like ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews for a recommendation instead of scrolling a results page. Being surfaced there comes from a consistent business identity and structured, factual content — which ScaleLocal builds into every site. See AI Solutions for more.

What are the AI automation tools?

The AI layer includes an AI receptionist that answers calls, website chat that responds instantly, automated follow-up that keeps leads warm, automatic review requests, and online booking. Together they make sure no lead slips through while you are working. See AI Automation for detail.

Do I have to manage the website myself?

No. ScaleLocal builds, hosts, and manages the website for you. You never touch the technical side — when something needs to change, you tell us and it is handled.

How do I get started with ScaleLocal?

Start with a free website demo. Tell us about your business and we will build a demo within 48 hours so you can see the work before deciding anything. Begin on the Get Started page.
