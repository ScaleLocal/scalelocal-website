Common questions

What does it sound like?

Natural and conversational — not robotic. It uses your business name, knows your services and hours, and speaks the way a well-trained receptionist would. Most callers don’t realize it’s AI.

Can I keep my current phone number?

Yes. We set up a dedicated ScaleLocal line that forwards to your cell. Your existing number stays yours. Customers can call either one.

What if the AI can’t handle a question?

It transfers the call to you immediately. You set the rules — certain questions, VIP callers, or urgent situations always route to your phone.

How quickly can it be live?

72 hours from when you answer 5 simple questions about your business. Most of the build is automated — we just need to know your services, hours, and how you want calls handled.

Is the $997 setup fee negotiable?

No. It covers the full build, configuration, call testing, and go-live. We don’t cut corners on the setup because a bad first impression on a caller costs you more than $997.

Can I cancel?

Yes. The standalone AI Receptionist is month-to-month after the setup fee. Cancel anytime with 30 days notice.
