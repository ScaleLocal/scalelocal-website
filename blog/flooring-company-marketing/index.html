How do flooring companies get more customers?

7 min read

Flooring companies get more customers by winning the online research that happens before anyone walks into the showroom. Homeowners browse styles and read reviews online, then visit in person to decide — so strong local SEO, a rich photo gallery, and a deep review wall drive both showroom traffic and booked installs. Flooring is visual and local, so being the company they find and trust online is what fills the showroom floor.

Flooring sits in an interesting spot. It’s a big, visual purchase a homeowner researches heavily online — scrolling styles, comparing hardwood against luxury vinyl, reading reviews — and then, more often than most trades, they want to walk into a showroom, touch the samples, and see it under real light before they commit. That means flooring marketing has to win two stages: the online research that builds the shortlist, and the showroom visit that closes the deal. Nail the first and you fill the second. Here’s how flooring companies do it.

Win the local search that fills the showroom

Before a homeowner visits any showroom, they search — “flooring store near me,” “hardwood flooring installation,” “LVP near me.” With 46% of searches carrying local intent and the top-three map results capturing about 44% of clicks, your local ranking decides whose showroom makes the shortlist. A flooring company buried on page two simply doesn’t get the visit.

This is where showroom flooring businesses have an edge if they use it: a physical location is a powerful local-ranking signal. Make it count:

  • A complete Google Business Profile with your showroom address, hours, product lines (hardwood, LVP, tile, carpet, laminate), and interior photos so browsers can preview the space.
  • Service and product pages on your site that rank for specific searches, not just “flooring.”
  • Consistent business details everywhere. If your showroom isn’t surfacing in local results, diagnose it with why your business isn’t showing up on Google, and cover the basics with a local SEO checklist.

Sell with photos — flooring is intensely visual

Few purchases are as visual as flooring. A homeowner wants to see wide-plank oak in a real living room, herringbone tile in an actual kitchen, LVP that looks convincing in someone’s home — not a manufacturer’s catalog shot. Photos of your real installs are your most persuasive content, because they let a homeowner picture the floor in their own space.

Build a deep gallery organized by product and room on your site, and load it onto your Google profile, where Google’s AI features now pull directly from your photos. Shoot every completed install. Over time that portfolio does the early selling — it’s what turns an online browser into someone who drives to your showroom specifically.

Reviews build the trust that earns the visit

A homeowner won’t drive across town to a showroom they don’t trust, and they decide who to trust by reading reviews. With 97% of consumers reading reviews and about 90% trusting them like a personal recommendation, your review wall is doing the convincing before anyone walks in. Reviews are also roughly 20% of local ranking, so a steady stream both ranks your showroom higher and reassures the shopper choosing where to go. Ask every install customer when they’re standing on their gorgeous new floor — our guide to getting more reviews makes it simple.

Capture every inquiry — online and on the phone

Flooring customers reach out in waves — a call to ask about pricing, a form to book a measure, a question about lead times. Miss those and you lose the visit. The average home-service business misses 30 to 40% of calls during peak hours, and a flooring shop with one or two people running the floor can’t catch every call during a busy Saturday. An AI receptionist answers and books the measure appointment so the inquiry becomes a job instead of a missed opportunity — the cost of the ones you miss is in what missed calls are costing your business.

Own your leads — and your past customers

Buying flooring leads from shared platforms is the usual bad bargain: sold to three to five companies, closing at just 10 to 15%, and building nothing you keep. Invest instead in the local ranking, photo gallery, and reviews that compound and keep driving showroom traffic. We map the options in the best lead sources for contractors. And don’t forget the warmest source of all: a homeowner who loves their new floor refers friends and comes back for the next room. Past customers and referrals are the most reliable lead source there is — the family whose living room you floored last year is your kitchen and bedroom job this year.

Turn the showroom into a content and review engine

Your physical showroom is an asset most flooring companies underuse online. Every customer who walks out happy with a new floor is a review waiting to be asked for and an install photo waiting to be shot. Build the habit: capture the finished job, ask for the review while they’re thrilled, and feed both back into the profile and gallery that brought them in. Do that consistently and the showroom and the online presence start reinforcing each other — the floors you install become the photos that rank you, and the reviews that earn the next visit. That loop is what separates a flooring company that’s found from one that’s overlooked.
