What local SEO covers

Being found is the first step to being chosen

A customer cannot call a business they never see. Local SEO is the work that puts you in front of people searching for your service in your area.

01 · Google Business Profile

The listing that decides local visibility.

For: any local business that depends on customers nearby.

Your Google Business Profile is often the first thing a local customer sees. We optimize it fully — every field completed, categories correct, services listed, and questions answered — so it works as hard as it can for you.

  • Complete, accurate, fully optimized Google Business Profile
  • Correct categories and service listings
  • Consistent business information across the listing and your site
02 · On-site and technical SEO

The site itself has to be readable and fast.

For: a business whose website exists but never ranks.

Search engines rank what they can read and trust. We handle the on-page and technical work — page structure, speed, schema markup, and content — so each page is clear about what you do and where you do it.

  • On-page optimization — titles, structure, and content
  • Schema markup so engines understand your business
  • Fast, well-structured pages built for local search terms
03 · AI search and voice visibility

Customers now ask AI and voice assistants too.

For: a business that wants to be found wherever customers search next.

More people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, or a voice assistant for a local recommendation. Getting surfaced there comes from the same foundation — a consistent business identity and structured, factual content across the surfaces those engines read.

  • Content structured so AI engines can cite your business
  • Consistent business identity across the sources voice assistants use
  • A foundation built for Google, Bing, and AI search at once

Local SEO works best with the site built for it

Local SEO and your website are not separate projects. The fastest results come when the website is structured for search from the start — which is why ScaleLocal handles both together rather than optimizing around a site that fights the effort.
