Missed calls = missed revenue

62% of calls to local businesses go unanswered. Every missed call is a customer who calls the next business on Google instead. If your average job is worth $500 and you miss just 3 calls a week, that’s $6,000/month walking away.

Estimated cost: $3,000–$8,000/month in lost revenue

Your website isn’t converting visitors into calls

Most local business websites are digital brochures — they look fine but don’t do anything. No chat, no instant booking, no clear call-to-action above the fold. Visitors land, look around, and leave. The average local site converts under 3% of visitors into leads. A conversion-optimized site hits 8–18%.

Estimated cost: 60–80% of your website traffic wasted
3
Your reviews aren’t growing fast enough

Google’s algorithm rewards review velocity — not just the total count. If your top competitor is getting 5 new reviews a month and you’re getting 1, you’re losing ground on the map pack every single week. Most businesses don’t have a system to ask for reviews automatically after every job.

Impact: Losing map pack position month over month
4
No one’s actively managing your Google presence

Google Business Profile posts, Q&A responses, photo updates, category optimization — these are the signals that tell Google you’re active and relevant. If your profile hasn’t been touched in months, Google is slowly demoting you in favor of competitors who are actively managing theirs.

Impact: Declining visibility in local search results
5
You’re invisible to AI search

When a potential customer asks ChatGPT, Google Gemini, or Perplexity for a recommendation in your area, you probably don’t show up. Most of your competitors don’t either — yet. The businesses that invest in AI search visibility now will own this channel before anyone else realizes it matters.

Opportunity cost: First-mover advantage window closing
