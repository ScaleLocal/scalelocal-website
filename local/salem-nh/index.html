Salem is a busy border-corridor town

Salem is a town of about 30,000 directly on the I-93 corridor at the MA/NH border. The economy is anchored by retail (Tuscan Village, the former Rockingham Park site redeveloped into one of the largest mixed-use projects in NH), trades, restaurants, and a steady residential service-business demand. The two big customer flows are local NH residents and Massachusetts commuters and shoppers crossing the border — the no-sales-tax dynamic pulls real volume into Salem retail.

For a Salem service business, that mix means the local SEO setup has to handle both NH-local searches and cross-border MA searches simultaneously. A Methuen homeowner searching for an HVAC company sees Salem options because the towns share the border. A Salem resident searching the same thing is looking at the same set of competitors. The businesses that win are the ones whose fundamentals are clean for both audiences.

What it takes to win in Salem

Salem is moderately competitive in most service categories — less saturated than Manchester or Nashua, more than smaller NH towns. The category density depends a lot on the trade: HVAC and plumbing have established names with budgets; some other categories have real room for new entrants with strong fundamentals.

The win condition is the standard one done well: fast modern website, complete Google Business Profile, recent reviews, naming Salem and nearby towns honestly in the content, and AI lead capture for missed calls and after-hours. The Tuscan Village expansion has brought more retail and restaurants to the area, which is also driving more search volume across categories.
