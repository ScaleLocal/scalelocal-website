Methuen sits between two markets and has its own character

Methuen is a city of about 53,000 people, directly north of Lawrence and just south of the NH border. The mix is residential, commercial, and small industrial — with the Methuen Square area, East Methuen, and the Searles district anchoring the older parts of town, and newer development pushing along the Route 213 and Route 110 corridors.

The local service business mix follows the housing: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, contractors, auto services, restaurants, salons, and a solid layer of professional offices.
