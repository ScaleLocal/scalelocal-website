Lawrence is a small-business city with its own rules

Lawrence is a city of about 89,000 people, one of the most diverse in Massachusetts, with a large Latino population and a small-business density that punches far above its size. Restaurants, salons, trades, retail, auto services, and corner professional offices fill the neighborhoods of South Lawrence, North Lawrence, Tower Hill, the Plains, and the historic mill district.

The Lawrence buyer often searches in Spanish or in a mix of English and Spanish. The classic local SEO advice written for the average American suburb partially applies and partially does not. Reviews in Spanish are common, Google Business Profile descriptions in both languages help, and a bilingual or Spanish-language landing experience can be a real advantage for the businesses willing to invest in it.

A lot of Lawrence small businesses have not done the basic online work — the Google Business Profile is unclaimed, the website is on a free template or does not exist, the missed calls go to voicemail. What's changing in 2026 for Lawrence businesses

There is still a lot of room for Lawrence businesses to claim positions in those summaries before the category fills up.

AI chat widgets that handle both languages, and AI receptionists for after-hours, are starting to show up in the bigger Lawrence operations and pulling work from the smaller ones that have not adapted.
