At least 100 for meaningful results. The sweet spot is 500–2,000. If you have fewer than 100, we’ll let you know before you pay.

No. The messages are personal, warm, and offer-forward — not spammy. They come from your business name, not a marketing company. Every message includes an easy opt-out. Response rates prove people appreciate being reached out to.

What if my list is messy?

That’s expected. We clean it — remove duplicates, fix formatting, flag bad numbers. You get the cleaned list back as part of the deliverable whether or not you continue with ScaleLocal.

How fast will I see results?

First responses typically arrive within 24–48 hours of the first message. Most campaigns see 80% of total responses within the first 10 days.

Any human reply: interest, booking request, referral, decline, or opt-out. Bounced messages and auto-replies do not count. Every response is documented in your report.
