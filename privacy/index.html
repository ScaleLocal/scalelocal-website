Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ScaleLocal (“Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), operated by Matt Yassen with principal offices in Tewksbury, Massachusetts, collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you visit our website, use our services, or interact with our AI-powered tools. By using our website or services, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information You Provide Directly: When you fill out a form, request a digital audit, use our ROI calculator, subscribe to a plan, or contact us, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, business name, business address, industry, and other information relevant to the services you are requesting.
  • Information Collected Automatically: When you visit our website, we may automatically collect technical data including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, time spent on pages, and general geographic location. This is collected through standard web analytics tools and cookies.
  • Information from Third-Party Sources: In the course of delivering our marketing services, we may collect publicly available business information from sources including Google Business Profiles, public business directories, government records, and publicly accessible websites. This information may include business names, addresses, phone numbers, hours of operation, customer reviews, and other data that is freely accessible to the general public.
  • AI Interaction Data: When you or your prospects interact with ScaleLocal-managed AI tools (including AI chat assistants and AI receptionists), those interactions may be recorded, transcribed, and stored. This includes text-based chat conversations, voice call transcripts, and any information voluntarily provided during those interactions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To deliver the marketing services you have purchased, including website development, SEO, advertising management, lead generation, AI tool configuration, and reporting.
  • Lead Generation & Management: To capture, qualify, and route leads to your business through the channels we manage on your behalf (website forms, phone calls, chat, SMS, and direct messages).
  • AI Tool Operation: To configure, operate, and improve AI chat assistants and AI receptionists that interact with your prospects on your behalf. AI interaction data is used to ensure accurate responses and to improve service quality.
  • Communication: To contact you regarding your account, service updates, performance reports, billing, and support.
  • Analytics & Improvement: To analyze website traffic, user behavior, and service performance in order to improve our website and services.
  • Compliance & Legal: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes, and to protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property.

3. Public Data Collection & Usage

As part of our marketing and lead generation services, ScaleLocal may gather publicly available information about businesses and individuals from sources accessible to the general public. This includes but is not limited to: Google Business Profiles, Yelp, public websites, social media profiles, government databases, and online business directories.

We do not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or currentness of any publicly sourced data. Public data is used solely for the purpose of delivering contracted marketing services, including competitive analysis, market research, lead enrichment, and prospect outreach. We do not sell publicly gathered data to third parties.

4. AI-Specific Data Practices

ScaleLocal deploys AI-powered tools on behalf of our clients, including:

  • AI Chat Assistants: Text-based chatbots embedded on client websites that engage with visitors, answer questions, capture lead information, and route inquiries.
  • AI Receptionists: Voice-based AI systems that answer phone calls, qualify callers, book appointments, and capture contact information.

Data collected by AI tools (names, phone numbers, email addresses, service inquiries, appointment preferences, and conversation transcripts) is stored within ScaleLocal’s CRM platform and is accessible to the client whose business the AI represents.

AI-generated responses are not guaranteed to be accurate. While we configure AI tools to provide helpful and accurate information based on the data provided by the client, AI systems may occasionally produce incorrect, incomplete, or unintended responses. ScaleLocal is not liable for decisions made by prospects or customers based on AI-generated outputs.

AI interaction data is not used to train third-party AI models. Conversation data collected by ScaleLocal’s AI tools is used solely for service delivery and quality improvement within ScaleLocal’s platform. We do not share AI interaction data with third-party AI providers for the purpose of model training.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies, pixels, and similar tracking technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality (session management, security).
  • Analytics Cookies: Used to understand how visitors interact with our website (page views, traffic sources, user behavior). We may use Google Analytics or similar tools for this purpose.
  • Advertising Cookies: If applicable, used to deliver relevant advertisements and measure campaign performance through platforms such as Google Ads and Meta (Facebook/Instagram).

You may control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We use third-party platforms to deliver our services, including CRM systems, email/SMS delivery platforms, advertising platforms (Google, Meta), analytics tools, and payment processors (Stripe). These providers receive only the information necessary to perform their specific functions and are contractually obligated to protect your data.
  • Client Access: If you are a prospect interacting with a ScaleLocal-managed AI tool or website, the client (business owner) whose services you are inquiring about will have access to your inquiry data, including your name, contact information, and conversation transcript.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of ScaleLocal, our clients, or others.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Specifically:

  • Client account data: Retained for the duration of the active service engagement and for a period of two (2) years following termination for record-keeping and legal compliance purposes.
  • Lead and prospect data: Retained within the client’s CRM account for the duration of the service engagement. Upon termination, data may be exported per the Terms of Service (Section 7).
  • AI interaction transcripts: Retained for a maximum of twelve (12) months for service quality and dispute resolution purposes, then permanently deleted unless a legal hold requires extended retention.
  • Website analytics data: Retained in accordance with the policies of the analytics provider (e.g., Google Analytics default retention settings).

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encrypted data transmission (SSL/TLS), access controls, secure password policies, and regular security reviews. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: You may request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Deletion: You may request that we delete your personal information, subject to legal and contractual retention requirements.
  • Opt-Out of Communications: You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us directly. Opting out of marketing communications does not affect service-related communications.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the information provided in Section 13 below. We will respond to verifiable requests within thirty (30) days.

10. Massachusetts & State Privacy Laws

ScaleLocal complies with the Massachusetts Data Privacy Law (201 CMR 17.00) and other applicable state privacy regulations. We implement a written information security program (WISP) as required by Massachusetts law to protect personal information of Massachusetts residents. If you are a resident of a state with additional privacy rights (such as California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may exercise those rights by contacting us.

11. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 18, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

12. Changes to This Policy

ScaleLocal reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Active clients will be notified of material changes via email. Continued use of our website or services following the posting of changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
